# Weekly promo reel and email, 2026-09-14

**Anchor fact used:** Two new members joined this week, but zero new dogs were added (totals now 14 members, 10 dogs, 1 waitlist, 7 distinct breeds owned, unchanged from last week). Rather than force a growth or diversity angle that the numbers do not support this week, this one leans into a real lifecycle nudge: new members who have not added a dog yet, alongside a check in for everyone else.

**Style note:** No dashes. Sentence case throughout, per Stefan's standing feedback.

## Reel script (30 to 45 seconds)

**Hook (0 to 3 seconds)**
Voiceover: Two new members joined this week. One step is still open.
On screen: New members. One step to go.

**Beat 1**
Voiceover: Every match starts with a dog profile, health verified from day one.
On screen: Add your dog. Start your match.

**Beat 2**
Voiceover: No puppy mills. No exceptions. Same standard for every dog.
On screen: No puppy mills. No exceptions.

**Beat 3, call to action**
Voiceover: If you just joined, this is your next step. If you have been here a while, check on your matches.
On screen: Add a dog. Check your matches. Invite a friend.

**End card**
On screen: Forming Paws logo.
