# Weekly promo reel and email, 2026-09-28

**Anchor fact used:** Growth was flat this week (1 new member, 0 new dogs; totals unchanged elsewhere at 16 members, 12 dogs, 1 waitlist, 8 distinct breeds). Rather than force a stats hook on a quiet week, this one pivots to an educational angle: what health verification actually means on the platform, reinforcing the core differentiator instead of a growth number. Keeps the claim at the same level of specificity already used elsewhere (documented health records before listing, no puppy mills) without inventing new process detail.

**Style note:** No dashes. Sentence case throughout, per Stefan's standing feedback.

**Hook (0 to 3 seconds)**

On screen: What health verified actually means.

**Beat 1**

**Beat 2**
Voiceover: That is how we keep puppy mills out. Not just a promise, a standard.
