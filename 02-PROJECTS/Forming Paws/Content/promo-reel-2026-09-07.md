# Weekly promo reel and email, 2026-09-07

**Anchor fact used:** Seven distinct breeds are now represented among members' dogs, up from six last week (out of 112 in the catalog). Growth was modest this week (1 new member, 1 new dog, totals now 12 members, 10 dogs, 1 waitlist), so this week leans on breed diversity instead of repeating the growth framing from the last two weeks. Continues the trust seal voice from the flyer for consistency.

**Style note:** No dashes. Sentence case throughout, per Stefan's standing feedback.

## Reel script (30 to 45 seconds)

**Hook (0 to 3 seconds)**
Voiceover: Seven breeds. One trust seal.
On screen: Seven breeds. One seal.

**Beat 1**
Voiceover: From pit bulls to poodles, every dog here carries the same standard.

**Beat 2**
Voiceover: Health verified. No puppy mills. No exceptions.
On screen: No puppy mills. No exceptions.

**Beat 3, call to action**
Voiceover: See who is here. Your match might already be waiting.
