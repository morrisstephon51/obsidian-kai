# Weekly promo reel and email, 2026-11-02

**Anchor fact used:** Third consecutive week with identical, completely flat numbers (15 members, 11 dogs, 1 waitlist, 7 distinct breeds, 1 total match, 7 total messages, zero new activity anywhere). Since content alone has not moved these numbers across eight weeks of varied angles, this week changes the format itself: instead of another one way promotional push, it asks subscribers a direct question and invites a reply. Useful content for them, and a real feedback signal for Stefan on why matching has not been happening.

**Flag for Stefan (brief):** same numbers as the last two weeks exactly, no change in either direction. Given this is the third week trying a new content angle without any movement, it may be worth treating this as a product or onboarding question rather than a content one. This week's email asks members directly, so real replies (if any come in) may be more useful than another data pull next week.

**Style note:** No dashes. Sentence case throughout, per Stefan's standing feedback.

## Reel script (30 to 45 seconds)

**Hook (0 to 3 seconds)**
Voiceover: We have a question for you.
On screen: Quick question for you.

**Beat 1**
Voiceover: If you have not matched yet, we want to know why.
On screen: What is stopping you from matching?

**Beat 2**
Voiceover: Not enough dogs your breed? Not sure how to start a conversation? Just busy? Tell us.
On screen: Reply and tell us. We are listening.

**Beat 3, call to action**
Voiceover: Hit reply. A real person will read it.
On screen: Reply to this email. Check your matches.

**End card**
On screen: Forming Paws logo.

## Shot list

1. Text card on brand background, no footage needed. Text: Quick question for you.
2. Text card, same style. Text: What is stopping you from matching?
3. Screen recording of browse cards or the matches page, slower pace than usual. Text: Reply and tell us. We are listening.
4. Candid shot of a dog and owner together, warm natural light, phone filmable. Text: none, let it breathe.
5. Close up of the verification seal graphic from the flyer. Text: none.
6. Text card. Text: Reply to this email.
7. End card, logo centered, CTA button mockup. Text: Check your matches.
