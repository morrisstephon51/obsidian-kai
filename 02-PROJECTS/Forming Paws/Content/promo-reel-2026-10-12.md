# Weekly promo reel and email, 2026-10-12

**Anchor fact used:** Third flat week in a row (0 new members, 0 new dogs; totals unchanged at 16 members, 12 dogs, 1 waitlist, 8 distinct breeds). Checked matches and messages too: only 1 total match and 7 total messages have ever happened on the platform, none this week. That is too weak to spin into a promotional stat, so this week avoids growth and engagement numbers entirely and leans on honest founding member positioning instead, with invite a friend as the direct ask since referrals are the realistic lever right now.

**Flag for Stefan (not for the email, just worth knowing):** growth has been flat for three consecutive weekly runs now, and total platform activity is very thin (1 match, 7 messages lifetime across 16 members and 12 dogs). Worth a look at whether something upstream of this weekly email (onboarding, visibility of the match feature, a smaller true active base than the member count suggests) needs attention.

**Style note:** No dashes. Sentence case throughout, per Stefan's standing feedback.
