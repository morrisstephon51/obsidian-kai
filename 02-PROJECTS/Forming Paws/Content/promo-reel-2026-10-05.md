# Weekly promo reel and email, 2026-10-05

**Anchor fact used:** Second flat week in a row (0 new members, 0 new dogs; totals unchanged at 16 members, 12 dogs, 1 waitlist, 8 distinct breeds owned out of 112 in the catalog). Rather than repeat last week's "what health verified means" angle, this one flips the breed diversity framing from an earlier week: instead of celebrating breeds already represented, it invites members toward the 104 breeds in the catalog with no match on the platform yet. Real catalog math (112 minus 8), not a fabricated stat.

**Style note:** No dashes. Sentence case throughout, per Stefan's standing feedback.

## Reel script (30 to 45 seconds)

**Hook (0 to 3 seconds)**
Voiceover: There are 104 breeds here with no match yet.
On screen: 104 breeds. Zero matches yet.

**Beat 1**

On screen: No puppy mills. No exceptions. Any breed.
