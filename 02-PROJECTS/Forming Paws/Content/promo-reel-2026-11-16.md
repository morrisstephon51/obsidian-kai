# Weekly promo reel and email, 2026-11-16

**Anchor fact used:** Fifth consecutive week with identical numbers across every metric (15 members, 11 dogs, 1 waitlist, 7 distinct breeds, 1 total match, 7 total messages, zero new activity anywhere). This week's angle is profile optimization, a practical tip distinct from the earlier add a dog nudge (getting listed at all) and the after the match piece (what to say once matched): making an existing profile actually work, a clear photo and an honest bio.

**Flag for Stefan, more direct this time:** this is the fifth straight week of identical numbers, and eleven distinct content angles across ten weeks have not moved them even slightly. At this point the evidence points away from a content problem and toward something upstream, onboarding, visibility, or a genuinely small active base. Worth deciding whether to keep this weekly cadence running as is, pause it until something changes on the product side, or shift it to a lighter check in instead of a full script and email every week. Not my call to change the trigger itself, just flagging that the current approach may have run its course for now.

**Style note:** No dashes. Sentence case throughout, per Stefan's standing feedback.
