# Weekly promo reel and email, 2026-09-21

**Anchor fact used:** Two new dogs joined this week (alongside 1 new member), pushing breed diversity to 8 distinct breeds out of 112 in the catalog, up from 7 last week. Totals now 15 members, 12 dogs, 1 waitlist. Two real facts (new dogs plus the breed milestone) combined into one hook, without claiming any causal link to last week's "add your dog" nudge since that can't be verified from the data alone.

**Style note:** No dashes. Sentence case throughout, per Stefan's standing feedback.

## Reel script (30 to 45 seconds)

**Hook (0 to 3 seconds)**
Voiceover: Two new dogs joined the pack this week.
On screen: Two new dogs. One trust seal.

**Beat 1**
Voiceover: That makes eight breeds represented here, and counting.
On screen: Eight breeds. Growing every week.

**Beat 2**
Voiceover: Every one health verified. No puppy mills. No exceptions.
On screen: No puppy mills. No exceptions.

**Beat 3, call to action**
Voiceover: Check out who is new. Your match might be one of them.
On screen: Check your matches. Add a dog. Invite a friend.

**End card**
On screen: Forming Paws logo.

## Shot list

1. Screen recording of browse cards scrolling past a couple of new profiles. Text: Two new dogs. One trust seal.
2. Screen recording of the breed dropdown or a breed count indicator. Text: Eight breeds. Growing every week.
3. Close up of the verification seal graphic from the flyer. Text: No puppy mills. No exceptions.
4. Candid shot of a dog and owner together, warm natural light, phone filmable. Text: none, let it breathe.
5. Screen recording of a chat notification appearing. Text: Say hello.
6. Screen recording of the invite a friend or share action. Text: Know someone who would fit right in?
7. End card, logo centered, CTA button mockup. Text: Check your matches.
