## Key Strategies

### 1. Increase Income
**Multiple paths:**
- Salary increase (raise, new job, new role)
- Side income (service, product, gig work)
- Investment income (savings, investments)
- Business income (service business, startup)

### 2. Optimize Spending
**Principles:**
- Cut what doesn't matter
- Keep what aligns with values
- Avoid lifestyle inflation
- Maximize savings rate

### 3. Build Capital
**Create compounding:**
- Save consistently
- Invest appropriately
- Reinvest returns
- Compound over time

### 4. Create Leverage
**Stop trading time for money:**
- Build business with systems
- Create passive income streams
- Leverage technology/automation
- Build intellectual property

---

## Decision Framework: Income vs. Spending

**Increase income vs. reduce spending?**

**Increase income when:**
- You have valuable skills to offer
- Market pays well for those skills
- You can scale (one job → multiple opportunities)

**Reduce spending when:**
- Spending is excessive
- You're financing things that don't matter
- Each dollar saved = less lifetime earnings needed

**Ideal: Do both**
- Build income aggressively (highest leverage)
- Spend thoughtfully (avoid waste)
