# Galaxy Grant — $2,500

**Apply at:** https://galaxyofstars.org/galaxy-grants/ · **Deadline:** Oct 31, 2026 · **Cost:** free · **Time needed:** about five minutes

## Read this first

This is structured more like a sweepstakes than a grant. Their own terms and conditions say the organizers "may use a Random Number generator to select a winner or group of winners without human bias," allow one entry per person, and require a free Galaxy of Stars account. There is no published scoring rubric, no business plan, no financials and no pitch.

So: enter it, and do not spend an evening writing for it. Winners are announced the week after the deadline.

## Eligibility

Open to women and minority entrepreneurs at any stage, including people still thinking about starting a business. Gender and ethnicity are self-reported dropdowns on the form. No minority business certification is required or mentioned anywhere on their site.
