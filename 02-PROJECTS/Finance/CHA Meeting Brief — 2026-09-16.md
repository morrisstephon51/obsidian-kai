## 2. Talking point: a third idea — raise it, don't pitch it
Introduce this as a gut-check, not a business you're bringing to launch: *"I have a third idea I want your read on before I put any real time into it — I'm not asking you to help me build it today."*

**The idea:** paid consulting for people with an active bench warrant for missing a court date on a non-violent, minor matter (traffic, ordinance violation, low-level misdemeanor) — helping them find out what's owed and get the warrant recalled instead of it turning into an arrest.

**The one thing to settle before this goes anywhere:** a non-attorney calling a clerk's office and acting on someone's behalf with a court — even just paying a fee for them — can cross into unauthorized practice of law (a real, chargeable offense in Illinois) depending on exactly how it's done. You've decided to keep this strictly administrative if you build it at all, which means:
- **In bounds (probably):** explaining in plain language what an FTA warrant is and how recall generally works — the same kind of public-information explainer The Plug AI's own content already does; helping someone find their case number, court, and what's actually owed (public record); and, only with the person's written authorization, physically paying a specific, undisputed fee on their behalf — the same thing a family member could already do for them.
- **Out of bounds:** filing anything with the court, speaking to a judge or clerk to argue the case, giving an opinion on whether someone is entitled to relief, or promising any outcome. No motion, no advocacy, no guarantee.
- **Non-negotiable regardless of the legal answer:** flat fee for the administrative work only, never a percentage or a "success fee" tied to the warrant actually being recalled — a judge decides that, not you, and pricing it like an outcome is the exact pattern (*"guaranteed for a fee"*) your own Sept 11 expungement content warns people to watch out for. Worth saying that out loud to yourself before you say it to her: hold this to the same bar you're asking your own audience to hold expungement mills to.
- **Ask Niquenya directly:** *"Before I build anything here, I need a straight answer on where the non-attorney line is in Cook County courts and municipal courts. Can you point me to a lawyer referral service, a law-school small-business legal clinic, or an SBDC that handles this kind of question?"* Don't take a client or a dollar for this idea until that answer exists in writing.
