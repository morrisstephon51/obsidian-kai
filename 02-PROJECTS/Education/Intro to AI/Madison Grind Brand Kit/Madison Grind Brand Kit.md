# Submission

## 1. Final Brand Brief

**MADISON GRIND BRAND BRIEF**

1. **Brand personality:** Warm, quick, witty, unfussy.
2. **Colors:**
   - Primary: Loop Navy, #1F2A44
   - Secondary: Sunrise Orange, #F26B3A
   - Accent: Morning Cream, #FFF4E6
3. **Typography:** Bold, rounded capital letters that feel friendly and are easy to read from across the counter.
4. **Logo concept:** The words MADISON GRIND stacked in bold navy capitals, with one small orange half sun sitting above the name. The sun is a nod to opening at six thirty in the morning, and the name stays the star.
5. **Taglines:**
   - Coffee first. Then class.
   - Up before your 8 AM.
   - Fair price. Fast cup.

# Process Log (not submitted)

## Step 1: Three brand directions

| Direction Name | Personality in Three Words | Color Palette | Logo Concept |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Early Bird Loop** | Warm, quick, bright | Sunrise orange, Loop navy, and morning cream. Feels like 6:30 am on Madison Street. | The name in bold letters with one small half sun above it, like the sun coming up. |
| **Study Break** | Relaxed, clever, friendly | Chalkboard green, mustard, and paper white. Feels like a campus hangout. | The name with a small speech bubble, like a friend saying hi from behind the counter. |
| **Madison Street Signal** | Steady, urban, witty | Signal red, charcoal, and off white. Feels like the city around the shop. | The name with a short bold underline, like a street sign or a train platform stripe. |

**Inspection:** three rows, four columns in order, no brown, and no beans, steam, or cups. The three directions are actually different from each other.

**Why I picked Early Bird Loop:** A tired student at 6:45 am would walk toward that orange. It's simple enough to print small, and navy and orange get away from the brown every coffee shop uses.

## Step 1 continued: First brand brief (before refinement)

1. **Personality:** Bright, energetic, friendly, driven.
2. **Colors:** Primary Sunrise Orange #F26B3A, Secondary Loop Navy #1F2A44, Accent Morning Cream #FFF4E6.
3. **Typography:** Bold, rounded capital letters that feel friendly and easy to read.
4. **Logo concept:** A bold orange sun with short rays rising behind the words MADISON GRIND, with a simple navy line of the Chicago skyline underneath. It should feel like the start of a new day in the Loop.
5. **Taglines:** "Rise and grind, Chicago." / "Fuel your big day." / "Chicago's favorite morning stop."

**What I found when I inspected it:**
- **Personality drifted.** The business brief says warm, quick, a little witty. "Energetic" and "driven" sound like a gym, not a friend behind the counter.
- **Logo concept has three elements:** sun with rays, skyline, and name. It won't read at one inch.
- **Invented fact.** "Chicago's favorite morning stop" is a claim that isn't in the brief.
- **Hype.** "Fuel your big day" sounds like a company talking to a customer. The brief says never use hype.

**Refinement I sent (same conversation):**
> Good. Change the personality words to match the business brief: warm, quick, a little witty, not corporate. Make the logo concept simpler so it has only two elements: the lettering and one small half sun. Remove the skyline and the rays. Keep the colors exactly as they are, but make navy the primary color so the name stands out. Rewrite the taglines so they sound like something a student would say to a friend, and remove anything that claims the shop is Chicago's favorite.

The final brief is in the Submission section above.

## Step 2: Logo inspection

| Check | First logo | Final logo |
|---|---|---|
| Spelled MADISON GRIND | Yes | Yes |
| Lettering plus one shape | **No:** sun and rays, name, skyline | Yes: name and half sun |
| Only three brand colors | Yes | Yes |
| Plain white background | Yes | Yes |
| Any bean, steam, or cup | No | No |
| One inch squint test | **Fails:** the name gets small and the navy letters blur on the orange | Passes |
| Is the name the star | **No:** the sun is the star | Yes |

**Refinement:** Keep the same lettering style and colors. Remove the rays and the skyline. Make the sun a small half circle above the name so the name is the main focus. Stack MADISON over GRIND so it reads bigger on a cup.

## Step 3: Mockup inspection

**Before refinement:**
- Logo on the cup and sign matches the file. ✅
- Name spelled right everywhere. ✅
- No extra text beyond MADISON GRIND. ✅
- Neighborhood shop, not a luxury lobby. ✅
- **Beige squares on the phone (#E8DCCB) are not a brand color.** ❌
- **The prompt asked for a small storefront sign, but the sign was bigger than the cup.** ❌

**Refinement:** Keep the same arrangement and lighting. Change the phone squares to only the three brand colors, and make the sign smaller.

**How it was made:** The logos are vector files built straight from the brand brief hex codes, and the mockup places those exact files, so the logo cannot drift. The mockup is a clean illustration, not photorealistic product photography.
