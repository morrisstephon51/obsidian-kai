This is your **highest-leverage short-term goal** because it:
- ✅ Unlocks career opportunities
- ✅ Enables higher income
- ✅ Increases credibility
- ✅ Supports financial independence timeline
- ✅ Creates new network opportunities

## Why This Matters

### Career Impact
- Many opportunities require degree
- Baseline credential for advancement
- Shows commitment and capability
- Opens doors to better-paying roles

### Financial Impact
- Bachelor's degree correlates with 50-80% higher lifetime earnings
- Enables transition to higher-paying fields
- Creates foundation for other ventures

### Psychological Impact
- Completes unfinished goal
- Increases confidence
- Proves you can commit and complete
- Supports sense of autonomy

### Strategic Impact
- Foundation for all other goals
- Frees bandwidth to pursue other opportunities
- Increases optionality
