# Life Vision

Your desired future: the life you're working toward.

---

## The Vision

**In 10 years, you want:**
- Financial independence and autonomy
- Meaningful work with real impact
- Ability to help others at scale
- Personal control over time and direction
- Enough freedom, capability, and resources to choose your path

---

## The Core Principle

> Having enough freedom, capability, and resources to choose your path while helping others.

---

## What This Means in Practice

### Financial Independence
- Income exceeds expenses (passive or leverage-based)
- No dependency on single employer
- Capital to pursue opportunities
- Ability to take risks

### Meaningful Work
- Work that aligns with your values
- Impact beyond just earning money
- Using your systems-thinking and helping nature
- Building something that scales

### Personal Control
- Authority over your schedule
- Control over what you work on
- Freedom to say no to things that don't align
- Agency in your life direction

### Helping at Scale
- Not just 1-on-1 resource navigation
- Systems that help many people
- Leveraging your knowledge to amplify impact
- Creating permanent solutions

---

## The Path to This Vision

### Current Phase: Foundation Building
**Survival → Stability → Leverage → Independence**

1. **Stability first** — Build stable income and credentials
2. **Acquire capability** — Education, skills, capital
3. **Develop leverage** — Systems, networks, knowledge
4. **Transition out** — Move toward independent ventures
