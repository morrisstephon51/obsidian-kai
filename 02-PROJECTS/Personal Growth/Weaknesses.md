# Weaknesses

Areas for growth. Your edge of competence.

---

## Identified Weaknesses

### ⚠️ Over-Analysis and Information Overload
**Tendency to research extensively before acting**
- Gather lots of information
- Want certainty before committing
- Can delay decisions waiting for perfect knowledge
- Information seeking instead of action-taking

*Impact:* Can slow progress, delay decisions

*Antidote:* 80/20 rule, good-enough decisions, iteration

### ⚠️ Difficulty Narrowing Focus
**Attracted to many opportunities simultaneously**
- Many interests compete for attention
- Hard to say no
- Spread thin across areas
- Depth limited by breadth

*Impact:* Can't compound results in any one area

*Antidote:* Clear commitment to highest-leverage activities, ruthless prioritization

### ⚠️ Tendency Toward Isolation
**Often work independently without external input**
- Don't ask for help easily
- Self-reliance becomes isolation
- Miss opportunities for collaboration
- Limit network effects

*Impact:* Slower growth, fewer opportunities

*Antidote:* Intentional networking, asking for help, finding accountability partners

### ⚠️ Information Seeking Instead of Action
**Research and learning feel productive, but aren't always**
- Can mistake learning for progress
- Comfort in preparation phase
- Difficulty with imperfect action
- Perfectionism delays execution

*Impact:* Miss opportunities by overthinking

*Antidote:* Bias toward action, iteration, learning by doing

### ⚠️ Periodic Pessimism
**Sometimes lose faith in possibility**
- Fatigue from constraints
- Pattern-recognition can highlight what's hard
- Doubt about whether goals are achievable
- Can demotivate progress

*Impact:* Reduced motivation, less persistence

*Antidote:* Reviewing past successes, connecting to vision, talking it out

---

## The Paradox

Many of your strengths create these weaknesses:
- **Curiosity** → analysis paralysis
- **Systems thinking** → seeing all problems
- **Resourcefulness** → accepting isolation
- **High standards** → perfectionism

---

## What NOT To Do

❌ Try to eliminate these weaknesses through willpower alone
❌ Ignore them and hope they go away
❌ Let them sabotage your goals
❌ Use them as excuses

---

## What TO Do

✅ Acknowledge them
✅ Build systems that compensate
✅ Get external accountability
✅ Take action despite imperfection
✅ Reflect and adjust

---

## Strategies for Each

| Weakness | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Over-analysis | Set decision deadlines, use 80/20 rule |
| Narrow focus | Create quarterly commitments, kill projects ruthlessly |
| Isolation | Find accountability partner, schedule collaboration |
| Information seeking | Set learning time-box, commit to action |
| Pessimism | Journal wins, review vision quarterly |

- What's the cost of each weakness?

- Who could help you stay accountable?
- What's one small change you could make this week?
