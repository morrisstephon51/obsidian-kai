## Skill Development Strategy

### Priority 1: Technical Skills
**Rationale:** Most valuable for career and independence

**Approach:**
- Pick one technology stack (JavaScript/React, Python, etc.)
- Learn through structured course or bootcamp
- Build projects to prove capability
- Use in work or side projects

**Timeline:** 6-12 months

### Priority 2: Business Skills
**Rationale:** Required for independent ventures

**Approach:**
- Learn through books, courses, mentorship
- Apply immediately in current role
- Study business models and operations
- Start documenting what you learn

**Timeline:** Ongoing, parallel with technical

### Priority 3: Leadership Skills
**Rationale:** Enables scaling impact

**Approach:**
- Lead small projects in current role
- Mentor others when possible
- Read about leadership
- Seek feedback

**Timeline:** Develop naturally as responsibilities grow
