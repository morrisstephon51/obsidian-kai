Resume tips

  • Lead each bullet with an outcome, then the action that produced it.
  • Mirror the language of the posting so screening tools find you.
  • Quantify scope: caseload, budget, headcount, throughput.
  • Keep it to two pages and drop anything older than fifteen years.
  • Save and send as a PDF named FirstName-LastName-Role.pdf.
