What counts as a "qualified" slate

A candidate who has cleared reference checks, a skills conversation, and a motivation check — not just someone who matches keywords on a resume.

How we define retention

Still in the role, by client report, twelve months after placement. Contract assignments that end on their pre-agreed date count as a completed placement, not a loss.

If a direct placement does not work out inside the guarantee window, we run the search again at no additional placement fee.
