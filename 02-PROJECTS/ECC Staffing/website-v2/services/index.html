For Clients

Every engagement model, explained plainly

From a two-week coverage gap to a confidential executive search, here is how each of our four service lines works and how to tell which one fits.

For surges, leaves, and project ramps

Contract Staffing

We staff a role on our payroll for a defined stretch of time — a maternity leave, a seasonal peak, a project with a start and end date. You get coverage without adding headcount; the worker gets a real paycheck and a recruiter checking in.

  • Fastest path to a filled seat — most slates land inside 48 hours
  • No conversion fee if the assignment simply ends on schedule
  • We handle payroll, taxes, and workers' comp for the duration

Evaluate fit on the job before you commit

Contract-to-Hire

The person works the role for an agreed trial period, still on our payroll, while your team sees the actual day-to-day fit. If it works, you convert them to your headcount. If it does not, there is no severance conversation to have.

  • Try before you buy — on real work, not a panel interview
  • Clear, pre-agreed conversion terms with no surprise fees
  • Most common path for roles where culture fit matters as much as skill

Confidential, fully-vetted searches for key roles

Direct Placement

For roles that go straight onto your headcount — leadership hires, specialized or credentialed positions, or searches you would rather not run publicly. We manage the full cycle: sourcing, screening, reference checks, and offer support.

  • Every direct placement carries our replacement guarantee
  • Confidential search option when a role cannot be posted openly
  • One dedicated recruiter owns the search start to finish

Compensation benchmarking and retention strategy

Workforce Consulting

When the question is not "who do I hire" but "how do I stop losing people," we bring the market data: live compensation benchmarks for your role and region, and a look at where your onboarding or scheduling is quietly costing you retention.

  • Benchmarking pulled from active searches, not a stale survey
  • Retention read-out tied to our 92% placement-retention standard
  • Delivered as a working session, not a slide deck you shelve

Not sure which fits?

Tell us the role and the timeline — we will recommend the right engagement model on the call.
