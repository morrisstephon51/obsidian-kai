## Core Career Strategy

## Likely Direction

### Field
**Technology, Operations, Healthcare-Adjacent, or Entrepreneurship**

Possible roles:
- Healthcare operations or management
- Customer success leadership
- Technical operations
- Community outreach leadership
- Entrepreneurship (service-based initially)

### Criteria for Right Role
✅ Builds valuable skills
✅ Increases income
✅ Enables learning
✅ Aligns with helping mission
✅ Creates optionality for next moves

❌ Avoid dead-end roles
❌ Avoid pure commission without base
❌ Avoid work that doesn't teach anything
❌ Avoid roles that trap you long-term

## Long-Term Direction (5+ Years)

### Vision
**Transition from employee to independent builder**

Possible paths:
- **Consulting/Service business** — Using expertise to help others
- **Technology business** — Building products
- **Healthcare-related venture** — Combining tech + healthcare
- **Leadership role** — Running operations/organization

### The Move
- Have stable income and credentials ✅
- Have significant capital ✅
- Have proven capabilities ✅
- Have network and opportunities ✅
- → Ready to transition

---

## Key Success Factors

### 1. Choose the Right Role Now
- **Teaches valuable skills**
- **Increases income**
- **Provides learning**
- **Creates optionality**

### 2. Commit Long Enough to Compound
- **At least 2-3 years** to build real expertise
- **Show growth and impact**
- **Create value consistently**
- **Then leverage into next opportunity**

### 3. Always Be Learning
- **Technical skills** — Stay current, expand
- **Business acumen** — Understand operations, finance
- **Leadership** — Develop people skills
- **Systems thinking** — Go deeper

### 4. Build Strategic Network
- **Mentors** — Learn from people ahead
- **Peers** — Mutual learning and support
- **Communities** — Professional development
- **Relationships** — Seeds for future opportunities
