Programs

Four disciplines, one company.

Students pick a main track and rotate through the others, so a dancer also learns to project a line and a singer learns to move.

Dance

Ages 5–17 · 3 levels

Ballet foundations, jazz, hip-hop, and contemporary. Technique first, then choreography.

  • Barre and floor work
  • Across-the-floor combinations
  • Showcase choreography

Theatre

Ages 6–17 · 3 levels

Improv, scene study, and script work. Older students direct short scenes for the younger cast.

  • Voice projection and diction
  • Character building
  • Blocking and stage directions

Voice

Ages 7–17 · 3 levels

Breath support, pitch, harmony, and musical theatre repertoire. Solo and ensemble.

  • Warm-ups and ear training
  • Two- and three-part harmony
  • Audition song prep

Music

Ages 6–17 · 3 levels

Keyboard and percussion basics, rhythm reading, and the pit band for showcase nights.

  • Rhythm and note reading
  • Group keyboard
  • Showcase pit band
Curriculum

From first bow to center stage.

Every student is placed by age and an informal first-week assessment, then moves up when they are ready, not when the calendar says so.

Level 1 · Opening Act

Ages 5–8

Movement games, rhythm, story theatre, and confidence. Classes run 45 minutes so attention stays high.

Level 2 · Ensemble

Ages 9–12

Real technique in each discipline, reading scripts and sheet music, and featured roles in showcases.

Level 3 · Company

Ages 13–17

Advanced repertoire, audition prep, student choreography, and mentoring the younger levels.

Weeks 1–3

Foundations

Placement, warm-up routines, and group rules.

Weeks 4–8

Skill building

Technique blocks and the showcase theme is announced.

Weeks 9–12

Rehearsal

Casting, choreography, and run-throughs.

Week 13

Showcase

Tech rehearsal, then the families' night.

    Questions

    Before you ask.

    Do you pick up from school?

    Placeholder answer. List partner schools and walking or van pickup details here.

    Does my child need experience?

    No. Every student starts with a placement week and is grouped by age and comfort level.

    What should they wear?

    Comfortable clothes they can move in and closed-toe shoes. Dance students get a shoe list after placement.

    Can we switch days mid-term?

    Yes, with one week's notice, as long as the new group has space.

    Are showcase tickets extra?

    Each family gets four free seats per showcase. Additional seats are first come, first served.
