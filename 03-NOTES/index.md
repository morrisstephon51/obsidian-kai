## How to Use These Notes

**Permanent notes** = One idea per note, written in your own words.
**Connection to Areas** = Many of these notes feed into project-based goals in `02-PROJECTS/`.

For example:
- `Strengths` → `02-PROJECTS/Personal Growth/Strengths`
- `Core Values` → `02-PROJECTS/Personal Growth/Core Values`
- `Skills` → `02-PROJECTS/Development/Skills`

This separation lets you:
- **03-NOTES:** Capture raw self-knowledge as it emerges
- **02-PROJECTS:** Refine and action that knowledge
