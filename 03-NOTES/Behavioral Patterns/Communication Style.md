# Communication Style

Stefan's defined brand voice — short declarative sentences, community-rooted, no corporate language, no dashes or hyphens, plain English, emotionally grounded in community impact — is explicit and consistently applied. This isn't a generic "professional tone" request; it's a specific, almost literary constraint set (notably, no dashes/hyphens is unusual and shows real attention to rhythm and readability for his audience).

In direct interaction with Claude, his style is different but related: he asks for "Gen Z," "encouraging, straightforward" tone for general interactions, but for strategic/advisory conversations he asks for the opposite — "brutally honest," "unfiltered," explicitly rejecting hedging and both-sides framing (as in this conversation's userPreferences). This is a deliberate code-switch between audience-facing warmth and strategy-facing bluntness.

The recurring instruction for "a clear direct answer, a concise step-by-step reasoning summary, alternative perspectives/solutions, and a practical action plan" shows he wants *structured* output even when the content is blunt — bluntness and structure aren't in tension for him, they're complementary.

Key recurring brand phrases: "the gap," "people like me / people the tech world forgot," and the closing signature "The plug is in."
