# Habits

**Documenting before doing.** Voice guidelines, brand identity systems, the five-document pre-development framework, the LLM Council charter — Stefan habitually writes down the rules/structure for an activity before (or instead of, in some cases) doing the activity itself.

**Naming and personifying systems.** "The Plug AI," "My Health My Power," "Architect Agent," "Grant Research Agent," RAZOR/VAULT/PULSE/FORGE, "Kai" — there's a consistent habit of giving named identities to systems, agents, and frameworks. This may aid clarity and motivation, but it's also a lot of naming/identity overhead for a one-person operation.

**Using AI collaboration as a structured, recurring practice** — not occasional tool use, but standing frameworks (LLM Council applies to "ALL interactions including Claude Code"), recurring mentorship-style engagements, and persistent identity ("Kai") across sessions. This is closer to a daily/weekly operating habit than ad hoc help-seeking.

**Researching before acting, sometimes extensively.** The grant research (identifying specific funders, cycle dates, framing requirements) and the mobile dev workflow exploration (iPad CLI options, VS Code Tunnels + Tailscale) both show a habit of thorough upfront research — valuable, but also another instance of the "preparation as activity" pattern.
