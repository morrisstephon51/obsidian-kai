# Personality Traits

**High agency, high output.** In a short window Stefan produced a brand system, content calendar, grant LOI, fiscal sponsorship setup, scholarship essay, and multiple software prototypes. This isn't someone waiting for permission or resources — he builds with what he has.

**Systems-thinker to a fault.** Almost every problem gets a framework: the LLM Council for decisions, the five-document pre-development framework for builds, structured multi-month plans for grants (the nine-month Woods Fund plan). This is a genuine strength for execution quality, but it also means simple decisions sometimes get system-ified before they get *made*.

**Identity-flexible / persona-comfortable.** Adopting "Kai" as an assistant identity, running structured "mentorship" engagements with AI, and building a four-persona council all show comfort treating AI interaction as role-played professional relationships rather than plain Q&A. This is unusual and suggests Stefan thinks in terms of *teams and roles* even when working solo.

**Resourceful under constraint.** The zero-budget framing isn't despair — it's a design constraint he keeps building around (fiscal sponsorship instead of incorporating, Open Collective instead of paid infrastructure, internal BigHeart tool-building instead of external contractors).

**Pattern-seeker, especially with data.** Noting that "church-specific content outperformed general AI content significantly" on LinkedIn and using that to redirect strategy shows someone who watches signals and adjusts — when he has bandwidth to look.
