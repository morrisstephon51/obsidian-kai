# Core Values

**Community over abstraction.** Stefan's brand language explicitly centers "the gap" and "people the tech world forgot" — not as marketing abstraction but as the actual people in his Cook County network: church leaders, health workers, first-gen students. He's chosen to build for a specific, named, geographically-rooted population rather than a generic addressable market.

**Plain language as a moral stance.** The brand voice guidelines (no corporate language, no dashes, short declarative sentences, plain English) aren't just style preferences — they reflect a belief that complexity and jargon are exclusionary, and that translation is itself the value he provides.

**Self-directed learning as identity.** Stacking certifications (Google IT Support, IBM IT Support, Google UX Design, Python) while pursuing a CS degree, plus exploring blockchain and piano sight-reading independently, shows someone who treats "knowledge as a personal hobby" — stated directly — and doesn't wait for institutional permission to learn something.

**Rigor and accountability to himself.** The LLM Council framework, the five-document pre-development requirement before any AI-assisted build, and the request for "brutally honest advisor" mode all point to a value placed on not fooling himself — even if, per [[The Person Behind the Questions]], the execution of that value is imperfect.
