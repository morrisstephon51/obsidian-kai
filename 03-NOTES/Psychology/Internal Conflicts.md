# Internal Conflicts

**Mission vs. money, unresolved.** Stefan wants the money to come from the mission (fund The Plug AI through grants and recognition for community work), but the fastest money likely comes from monetizing his *general* skills (training package sales, freelance automation/agent-building, the BigHeart comp adjustment). These aren't mutually exclusive, but the instinct to keep them fused may be slowing both down.

**Structure vs. speed.** The five-document framework and LLM Council exist because Stefan values rigor and doesn't want to "fool himself" — but zero-budget, pre-revenue ventures usually need speed and scrappy validation more than process maturity. Every hour spent on framework is an hour not spent on the Kresge proposal or the BigHeart conversation.

**Founder vs. employee identity.** He's simultaneously building The Plug AI as his own venture AND trying to get BigHeart to formally recognize his internal tool-building with a new title ("Digital Content and AI Systems Specialist") and comp. These pull in different directions — one says "I'm building my own thing," the other says "I want this employer to value me more." Both can be true, but the energy split is real.

**Breadth vs. depth, perpetually re-litigated.** The shift from broad exploration (blockchain, piano, multiple job applications) to a converged mission (The Plug AI) was real progress — but the *number of simultaneous workstreams* under that one mission has arguably grown rather than shrunk. The conflict between "I've found my focus" and "I'm still doing seven things at once" hasn't actually been resolved, just relabeled.
