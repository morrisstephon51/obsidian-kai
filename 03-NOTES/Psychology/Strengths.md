# Strengths

**Real, deployed proof of concept.** Most early-stage founders have a deck. Stefan has four church partnerships running an AI-assisted outreach system in production (My Health My Power). That's a massive differentiator and most people in his position don't realize how rare that is.

**Cross-domain credibility.** Healthcare ops + ed-tech + hospitality + client success + emerging CS/AI skills is an unusual combination that maps almost perfectly onto "person who can talk to a pastor, a community health worker, AND write the Python." Most technical people can't do the first two; most community-facing people can't do the third.

**Defined, consistent brand voice.** Many founders never nail their voice. Stefan has a documented, applied, consistent voice (short sentences, no corporate language, "the gap," "the plug is in") that shows up across LinkedIn content, brand guidelines, and grant materials.

**Technical building capability that's actually current.** Next.js, Supabase, Remotion, FAL, Pexels, Vercel deployment, Composio for agent automation, multi-agent orchestration (Architect Agent) — this is not surface-level. He's debugging cron job limits and migration conflicts, which means he's actually in the code.

**Grant research literacy.** Identifying Woods Fund Chicago, NSF SBIR, Google.org AI Opportunity Fund, DOL/WIOA, Illinois DCEO digital equity grants, and Kresge Foundation — and understanding *framing requirements* (e.g., Woods Fund needs community organizing framing) — is a skill most technical founders lack entirely.
