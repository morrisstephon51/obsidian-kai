# Mental Models

**"The gap" as a unifying frame.** Stefan repeatedly returns to the idea of a gap — between underserved communities and emerging tech, between his own background and traditional tech credentials, between where he is and where the funding is. This single metaphor structures both his brand messaging and, likely, his self-concept.

**Compounding context.** He explicitly applies "the same compounding-context approach" from a prior structured mentorship engagement to current work — the belief that sustained, structured AI collaboration over time produces a portfolio/asset base that compounds, rather than one-off outputs.

**Translation as the core skill.** His UVP is framed as the ability to "translate AI and emerging tech into real-world impact for underserved communities" — and crucially, to "build, design, teach, and deploy independently." This is a mental model of himself as a *full-stack translator*, not a specialist in any one layer.

**Pre-development as risk mitigation.** The five-document framework (PRD → System Design → Wireframes → Feature Breakdown → Master Prompt) reflects a model where unstructured AI-assisted building is seen as risky/wasteful, and structured planning de-risks it. This is a reasonable model for larger builds; applied to every build it can become its own form of friction.

**Proof-of-concept-as-everything.** My Health My Power (the BigHeart church outreach work) functions in Stefan's mental model simultaneously as: a job deliverable, evidence for a title/comp change, a Plug AI case study, and validation of the underlying community-AI thesis. One artifact, four jobs.
