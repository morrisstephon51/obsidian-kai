# Educational Goals

Stefan is pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science at Maestro University while simultaneously holding (or pursuing) Google IT Support, IBM IT Support, Google UX Design, and Python certifications. This is a deliberate "credential the existing skills" strategy — he's already building production systems (My Health My Power, content automation pipelines, multi-agent orchestration) and the formal education adds legitimacy and fills theoretical gaps.

He's also engaged in self-directed learning outside any formal program: foundational blockchain curriculum (completed at a simplified level) and exploration of high-ROI pattern-recognition methods for piano sight-reading. Both of these were pursued out of genuine interest ("knowledge as a personal hobby") rather than direct career necessity, though the blockchain interest could plausibly connect to future Plug AI curriculum content.

He also recently drafted a scholarship essay for Generations College's AI for Business program — another educational/credentialing avenue actively in motion alongside the degree and certifications.

The pattern across all of this: Stefan over-indexes on breadth of credentials/learning relative to what's strictly necessary for his stated near-term goals. This isn't necessarily bad — broad knowledge supports the "translator" mental model — but it's worth flagging as another instance of the additive-not-subtractive pattern (see [[Decision-Making Style]]).
