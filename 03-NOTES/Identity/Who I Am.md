# Who I Am

Stefan is the founder of The Plug AI, an AI literacy education platform aimed at underserved communities in Chicago's south suburban Cook County area — specifically faith leaders, community health workers, and first-generation students. He simultaneously holds a training development and design role at BigHeart Health, where he has built and deployed AI-assisted outreach systems across four church partnerships under the "My Health My Power" initiative. He is pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science at Maestro University while stacking IT and design certifications (Google IT Support, IBM IT Support, Google UX Design, Python).

He operates inside a self-built "LLM Council" framework (RAZOR, VAULT, PULSE, FORGE) — a structured way of forcing multiple critical perspectives onto every decision rather than accepting a single AI-generated answer at face value. He's also adopted the assistant identity "Kai" for this work, which signals he treats this collaboration as a long-term operating partnership, not a one-off tool.

His background is genuinely multi-disciplinary: healthcare operations (Mount Sinai Hospital), education technology (Apollo After Schools), client success, and hospitality management. That breadth is unusual and is part of what makes his positioning credible — he isn't a tech person cosplaying community work, or a community person cosplaying tech. He's done both.
