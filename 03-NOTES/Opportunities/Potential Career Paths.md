AI/automation consultant or freelance builder for nonprofits and faith organizations.** Directly leverages the demonstrated skill set (agents, automation pipelines, training design) and the already-explored $350-750 training package model. Hybrid: BigHeart (stabilized, better title/pay) + Plug AI (grant-funded, part-time/evenings) + occasional consulting.** This may actually be the most realistic medium-term reality regardless of which "official" path is chosen — and may be worth explicitly planning for rather than treating as a fallback.
