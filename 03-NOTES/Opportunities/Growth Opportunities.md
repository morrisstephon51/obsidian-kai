# Growth Opportunities

**Personal: narrowing without abandoning.** The biggest growth opportunity isn't a new skill or project — it's the meta-skill of *sequencing and stopping*. Stefan has more than enough capability and more than enough good ideas. The growth edge is in choosing 1-2 things to finish completely before starting the next, and getting comfortable that not-yet-started ideas are still safe (they don't disappear by waiting).

**Professional: becoming the recognized "case study" for community-rooted AI literacy.** If My Health My Power and The Plug AI's broader work get documented and publicized (which is already underway via LinkedIn and brand work), Stefan is positioned to become a go-to voice/example in a space (AI literacy for underserved communities, faith-based tech partnerships) that funders and institutions are actively looking for examples of. This is a growth opportunity that compounds — more visibility → easier grants → easier BigHeart case for recognition → more visibility.

**Financial: closing the gap between capability and cash flow.** Stefan's skills (training design, automation, agent-building) are currently most directly value-realized through The Plug AI's mission and BigHeart's internal recognition — both somewhat slow paths. The growth opportunity is building at least one fast-cash-flow channel (training packages, consulting) that doesn't require waiting on grants or HR processes.

**Personal: music as a growth area on its own terms.** The sheet-music barrier is a solvable problem (pattern-recognition methods were already explored). Growth here isn't instrumental to anything else — it's just a real, stated source of personal connection that's currently stuck. Worth treating as its own small project, separate from all professional frameworks.

**Educational: leveraging the CS degree completion as a credibility milestone**, especially if timed to coincide with a major Plug AI or BigHeart milestone (e.g., Kresge funding announcement, title change) — stacking credibility events rather than letting them land independently and get diluted.
