# Business Opportunities

**Reviving the done-for-you training package model ($350-750/package).** This was already designed (Training Asset Studio app, Claude-powered) and directly leverages Stefan's strongest professional skill (training design) plus his automation capability. Selling to nonprofits/churches adjacent to his existing network could generate near-term revenue without competing with The Plug AI's grant-funded mission — and could even become a feeder channel (clients become future Plug AI partners or referral sources).

**Packaging the content automation pipeline as a service.** The Next.js/Supabase/Remotion/FAL/Pexels pipeline built for The Plug AI's own content could be offered to other small nonprofits or churches that need social content but can't afford agencies. This is the same "translation" value proposition applied as a product.

**The Plug AI as a replicable model, not just a single-site program.** If My Health My Power's church-network model proves out, it could become a template offered to other dioceses/denominations/regions — turning a single grant-funded program into a licensable or fundable model with multiple funding sources.

**Grant-writing/fiscal-sponsorship-setup as a service for other small community orgs.** Stefan has now navigated Open Collective fiscal sponsorship and built a multi-funder grant pipeline with framing research. Many small community organizations need exactly this and can't afford consultants — this is adjacent, monetizable expertise gained as a side effect of building The Plug AI.

**Caution:** all four of these are *additional* opportunities. Per [[Bottlenecks]], the question isn't "are there more opportunities" — there clearly are — it's whether adding any of them right now helps or hurts. The training package model is the strongest candidate for "add now" specifically because it's fast, proven-in-design, and directly addresses the zero-budget constraint.
