# Leverage Points

Ranked by speed-to-impact, these are the moves that would do the most with the least additional effort:

**1. Have the BigHeart conversation now, if it hasn't happened.** Title change + comp adjustment for work already done is the single fastest path to addressing the zero-budget constraint, and it requires zero new external relationships. If this conversation is being delayed in favor of more preparation, that preparation has likely already passed the point of diminishing returns.

**2. Use My Health My Power as the headline asset in every funder/partner conversation.** A live, four-church deployment with named contacts is rare and powerful. Any grant application, pitch, or scholarship essay should lead with this, not bury it.

**3. Scope the LLM Council and five-document framework down to genuinely complex builds.** Reserve heavy process for things like the content automation pipeline or Architect Agent. Strategic moves (BigHeart ask, grant follow-ups, partner outreach) need a plan, not a council.

**4. Pick ONE additional revenue experiment and run it for 30 days — the training package model is the strongest candidate.** It's already designed, it's fast, and it's the most direct answer to "zero budget" that doesn't depend on a grant cycle or an employer's HR process.

**5. Apply the LinkedIn-data discipline everywhere.** Before adding the *next* agent or tool, ask: did the last one (Grant Research Agent, JobScout) produce a measurable result yet? If not, that's the signal to pause new builds and either fix or retire the existing ones.

**6. Protect music as the one non-strategic space.** Don't build a framework for it. Don't make it serve The Plug AI's brand. It's working precisely because it's the one thing not under a system — leave it that way.
