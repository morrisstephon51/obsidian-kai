# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI

Every faith leader I talk to says the same thing.

"We're not sure about this AI stuff."

I hear that. That is a real instinct. And it is worth listening to.

But here is what I also know.

Every well-funded nonprofit is using AI to write faster, serve more people, and stretch smaller budgets further.

Every organization with resources is getting more efficient.

And most of the communities I grew up in are still waiting to see how this plays out.

That waiting has a cost.

The gap is not about who trusts technology more. The gap is about who gets access to the tools and who gets left out.

People the tech world forgot have always had to work twice as hard for half the resources. AI does not automatically fix that. But knowing how to use it can start to close it.

That is why I built The Plug AI. For the churches. The community health workers. The first-gen students in south suburban Cook County.

Not to convince anyone that AI is perfect. To make sure the people doing the hardest work with the fewest resources are not the last ones to learn how to use it.

The plug is in.

If your faith community wants a free AI literacy session this fall, reach out. No tech background needed. We come to you.
