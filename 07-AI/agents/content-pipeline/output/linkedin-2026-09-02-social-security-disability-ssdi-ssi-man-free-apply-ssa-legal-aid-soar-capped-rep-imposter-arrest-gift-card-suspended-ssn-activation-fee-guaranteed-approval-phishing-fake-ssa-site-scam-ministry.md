His back won't hold, the diabetes has taken his feet and is coming for his eyes, and the doctor keeps saying the word "heart" in a tone he doesn't like. He can't do the only kind of work he has ever known, and he has told almost no one the whole truth, because he was raised that a man works, a man provides, and a man does not "go on disability." You know him. Nobody ever told him that Social Security Disability is money he paid for out of every single check for decades, an insurance he is entitled to draw on now that he can't work, not a welfare line and not charity.
