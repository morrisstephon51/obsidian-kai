# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI
**Date:** 2026-07-04
**Audience:** Faith community (pastors, deacons, church administrators, community health workers)
**Angle:** The church is already doing what AI promises. We just need to close the gap.
**Status:** Draft — ready for Stefan review

---

Your pastor already knows who in the congregation is struggling.

Your deacon knows which families are one paycheck from a crisis.

Your church secretary has been managing calendars, budgets, and outreach for decades without a single "digital transformation" budget.

These people are not behind.

They are the most trusted resource navigators in the community.

But the tech world never designed AI tools for them.

That is not an oversight. That is a structural choice.

And it has consequences.

When AI literacy only flows to people with degrees, corporate jobs, or $200 a month in software subscriptions... the people who need leverage the most get left further behind.

Faith communities are already doing the work.

Feeding neighborhoods. Sitting with the grieving. Showing up when every other system has failed.

The question is not whether AI belongs in spaces like these.

The question is who shapes how it gets used.

At The Plug AI, we start with the community.

Not after the gap closes.

Right now.

Get Connected. Get Skilled. Get into AI.

The plug is in.
