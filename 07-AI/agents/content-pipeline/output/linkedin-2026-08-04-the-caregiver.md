# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-08-04
# Angle: The caregiver. The daughter holding a parent's whole medical life in a folder she does not understand. AI as the thing that gives her an hour of her life back.

---

She keeps everything in one folder.

Her mother's medications. The appointment cards. The insurance letters that contradict each other. The lab results nobody sat down to explain.

She is a daughter, a mother, and a full time worker. She is also, quietly, a nurse, a secretary, and a translator she never trained to be.

She does this at night. After her own kids are down. Tired.

Here is what nobody told her.

She can take a photo of that confusing letter and ask what it means in plain words. She can list the pills and ask which ones should never be taken together. She can turn three pages of medical language into five sentences she can actually understand before the next visit.

Not to replace her doctor. To walk into that office already knowing the right questions.

The tool is free. It is on the phone in her pocket while she folds the laundry.

That is the gap. It was never that she could not learn it. It is that nobody who looks like her, in a room she already trusts, ever showed her.

The Plug AI brings real AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Harvey. Matteson. Dolton. Not a webinar. A room. Real problems. The folder on the kitchen table.

Caregiving is love doing hard math at midnight.

We are here to hand that love better tools.

The plug is in.
