# LinkedIn Post — Church as Literacy Anchor

---

Decades ago churches ran literacy programs.

Not because it was a ministry requirement. Because people needed to read. And the church was the most trusted place in the community.

Then ESL classes. Then GED programs. Then digital literacy.

Every time a new skill became essential to participating in society the church showed up.

AI is next.

It is reshaping how people apply for jobs. How they navigate healthcare. How their kids do homework. How landlords and employers make decisions about them.

If the people walking through your doors do not understand how it works they are already behind.

Faith communities have always been the bridge for people the system forgot.

This is the same call. Different technology.

The Plug AI partners with faith communities in south suburban Cook County to bring AI literacy workshops directly to your congregation.

No jargon. No hype. Practical training your people can use the same week.

The plug is in.

If your church or community org is ready, drop a comment or send me a direct message.
