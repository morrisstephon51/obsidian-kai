# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-07-19
# Angle: AI is already shaping members' lives — the question is who understands it

---

AI is already in your church.

Not in a robot in the pew.

In the apps your members use every day.

The job posting that filtered someone out before a human ever saw their resume.

The insurance denial letter nobody can decode.

The healthcare chatbot that said "consult a provider" instead of just answering the question.

AI made those decisions.

And most of the people affected never knew it.

That is the gap I keep talking about.

Not a gap in intelligence. Not a gap in willingness. A gap in who got the explanation and who did not.

Faith communities are different from every other institution trying to solve this.

They already have the trust.

They already have the room.

They already have people who show up for each other when things get hard.

The Plug AI is what happens when that trust meets real tools.

South suburban Cook County is where we start.

The plug is in.
