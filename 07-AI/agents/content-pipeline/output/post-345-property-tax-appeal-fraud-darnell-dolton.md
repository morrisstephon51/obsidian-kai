**They Said They'd Lower His Taxes. They Just Lowered His Trust.**

Darnell, 52, has owned his home in Dolton for 17 years.

Raised three kids there. Buried his mother from that front porch. Every month, rain or shine, the mortgage got paid.

Last spring, a mailer landed in his mailbox. "ILLINOIS PROPERTY TAX REDUCTION SPECIALISTS — We've reviewed your property and you may be OVERPAYING. Average savings: $1,200/year. No win, no fee*."

That asterisk, Darnell didn't notice.

He called the number. Smooth talker. Professional. Knew the exact assessed value of his home, his PIN number, his township. Said Cook County was over-assessing Black-owned homes — which Darnell knew was actually true. (It is. Studies have proven it.)

So he signed the contract. Paid the $450 "administrative filing fee." Got an email confirmation. Then…

Nothing.

Six months later, Darnell called the Cook County Assessor's Office. No appeal on file. No record of this company ever contacting them. The appeal window had closed.

The company's website? Gone.

Deacon Frederick at True Vine Missionary Baptist in Dolton had seen it before. "Darnell, you're not the first," he said quietly. "Third person this year alone from our congregation."

---

**Here's what Darnell didn't know — and what you need to know:**

**1. Filing a property tax appeal in Cook County is FREE.**
Go directly to assessor.cookcounty.gov. No company needed. The Assessor's Office has a Taxpayer Advocate and multilingual staff who will walk you through it.

**2. Legitimate tax appeal attorneys work on contingency.**
Real firms take a percentage of your actual savings — not a fee upfront. If someone asks for money before results, walk away.

**3. The data is real. The exploitation is real too.**
Yes, Cook County has historically over-assessed homes in Black communities. ProPublica documented it. The Assessor's Office has acknowledged it. But scammers weaponize that truth to build trust — then disappear.

**4. If you missed the appeal window, you are not done.**
Contact Prairie State Legal Services (1-888-301-1111) or Legal Aid Chicago (312-341-1070). They may still help through the Board of Review or the Illinois Property Tax Appeal Board (ptab.illinois.gov).

**5. Report the fraud.**
Illinois Attorney General Consumer Protection: 1-800-243-0618 | illinoisattorneygeneral.gov
FTC: reportfraud.ftc.gov
