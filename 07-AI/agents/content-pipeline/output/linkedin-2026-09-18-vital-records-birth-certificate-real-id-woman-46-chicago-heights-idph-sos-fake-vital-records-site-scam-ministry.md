**She Needed One Document. The Internet Gave Her Two More Scammers.**

Monique, 46, works at a medical supply warehouse in Chicago Heights.

Eleven years at the same company. Supervisor calls her "the one who shows up."

Three years ago, her basement flooded. She lost a lot. Among the things that didn't survive: her birth certificate.

She didn't think much about it — until this spring, when her employer sent a memo.

**All employees must present REAL ID-compliant identification by the new federal deadline.**

Her driver's license was standard. Not REAL ID. To upgrade at the Illinois Secretary of State, she'd need to show a birth certificate.

So Monique did what most of us do. She Googled it.

*"How to get Illinois birth certificate replacement"*

The first result — appearing above the state's own website — was professional. Blue header. State seal. "Official Illinois Vital Records Processing Service."

She ordered. Paid $79. Got a confirmation number and a case ID.

Three weeks later: nothing.

She called the number. Voicemail. No callback. She emailed. Automated reply. Then, buried in the follow-up: *"Expedited processing available for $49."*

She paid that too.

Nothing.

Sister Arlene Brown runs a resource desk in the lobby of Mount Olive Missionary Baptist in Chicago Heights — third Saturday of every month, 10 a.m. to noon. Just a folding table and a binder of phone numbers, but people find her.

When Monique sat down across from her, Arlene recognized the story before Monique finished telling it.

"Baby, that wasn't the state. That was a scam. I've seen it four times this year alone."

**Here's what Monique didn't know — and what you need to know:**

**1. Illinois birth certificates cost $15 — directly from the state.**
Go to dph.illinois.gov or call the IDPH Office of Vital Records at (217) 782-6553. If you were born in Illinois, IDPH is the source. No middleman, no processing fee, no case ID needed from a stranger's website.

**2. If you were born in Cook County, the County Clerk also maintains birth records.**
cookcountyclerkil.gov — same low cost, same legitimacy, no third party involved.

**3. Those polished "processing service" websites are exploiting the gap between what Google surfaces and what the government actually provides.**
Some do eventually mail you a document — weeks late, marked up 5× the real cost. Others collect your name, Social Security number, date of birth, and mother's maiden name — then disappear. The risk is not just money. That is the information needed to open a credit card in your name.

**4. REAL ID is free to apply for at any Illinois Secretary of State facility.**
ilsos.gov/realid lists exactly what to bring. The process takes one visit. No "expeditor" service exists. No fee shortens the line. Anyone charging to "guarantee" faster REAL ID processing is lying.

**5. If you need help navigating the document process — especially if you were born in another state:**
Illinois Legal Aid Online (illinoislegalaid.org) and BirthRight Chicago / ID Chicago help low-income Illinois residents work through vital records requests from any state, including navigating fee waivers for those who cannot afford the cost.

**6. Report fake document websites:**
FTC: reportfraud.ftc.gov
Illinois Attorney General Consumer Protection: 1-800-243-0618
