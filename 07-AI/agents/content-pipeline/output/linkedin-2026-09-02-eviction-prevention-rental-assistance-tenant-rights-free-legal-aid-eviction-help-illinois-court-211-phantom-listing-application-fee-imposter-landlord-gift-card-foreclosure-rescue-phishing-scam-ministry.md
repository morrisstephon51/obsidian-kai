Now there is a notice taped to her door and a court date printed on a page she has read ten times and still does not understand, and she has told no one — not her pastor, not her sister, not the woman she prays with every Wednesday — because she was raised that you keep your own roof over your head and you do not put your troubles out in the street. So she packs a few boxes "just in case," she lies awake doing the math, and she calls it handling it. You know her.
