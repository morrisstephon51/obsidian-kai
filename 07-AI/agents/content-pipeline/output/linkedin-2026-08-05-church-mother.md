# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-08-05
# Angle: The church mother / mothers board elder. The woman who has been there forty years, keeps the prayer list, calls the sick, and holds the whole care ministry in her memory. AI as the care-coordination help a hospital chaplaincy office pays staff for.

---

There is a woman in every church who knows before the pastor does.

She knows who went into the hospital Thursday. She knows whose son is back in trouble. She knows which widow has not had a real conversation in nine days, because she is the one who calls her.

We call her a lot of things. Mother. First Lady's right hand. The mothers board. Mostly we just know her name and lower our voice when we say it.

She keeps the prayer list. Not the printed one in the bulletin. The real one, in her head and in a spiral notebook by the phone. Who is sick. Who is grieving. Whose surgery is Tuesday. Whose baby came early. She remembers all of it, and she works it like a shift nobody scheduled.

She has done this for forty years. She has never once been paid.

Here is what the hospital across town has that she does not.

It has a chaplaincy office. A care coordinator. Software that flags who needs a follow-up call and when. A staff person whose whole job is to make sure no one falls off the list. That care is not deeper than hers. It is just resourced.

She can have that help too. For free. On the phone already sitting next to that notebook.

She can take the list in her head and turn it into a simple care sheet that shows who was called and who is still waiting. She can draft the sympathy note she never quite finds the words for, in her own voice, ready to sign. She can build a small phone tree so the visiting does not all land on her. She can write the get well message for the bulletin without staying up to do it.

Not to replace what she does. The prayer is hers. The knowing is hers. It gives her back the hours and makes sure nobody gets missed.

That is the gap. It was never that the church cared less than the hospital. It is that nobody who sits in her pew, in a ministry she has carried alone for decades, ever told her the tool was hers to use.

The Plug AI brings real AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Harvey. Matteson. Dolton. Not a webinar. A room. Real problems. Her actual notebook open on the table.

The people holding a community together are not behind. They were just never handed the tools the funded rooms take for granted.

We are here to hand them over.

The plug is in.
