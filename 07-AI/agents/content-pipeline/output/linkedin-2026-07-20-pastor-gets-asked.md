Your pastor is not an AI researcher.

But three people asked him about AI last month.

He gave the best answers he could.

But nobody gave him the tools to give better ones.

That is not a reflection of your congregation. That is a reflection of who the AI industry decided to teach.

The people building these tools are not building them for Harvey. Not for Matteson. Not for Dolton. They are building them for someone else and trusting that the knowledge will trickle down.

It does not trickle down.

It has to be brought in.

The Plug AI brings practical AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Not a webinar. Not theory. Real tools. Real conversations. In a room where people already trust each other.

Your pastor should not have to figure this out alone.

The plug is in.
