# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-08-05
# Angle: The Sunday school / Christian education teacher. The volunteer who prepares a lesson every week for a room of kids, tracks who came, and keeps up with the families. AI as the curriculum-and-classroom help a funded school district pays a whole department for.

---

Every Saturday night, there is a woman at her kitchen table with a workbook, a glue stick, and thirteen kids to teach in the morning.

She is the Sunday school teacher. Nobody trained her for it. The last teacher moved away, somebody said her name, and she said yes.

So every week she does the whole job. She reads the lesson. She figures out how to explain it to a six year old and a ten year old sitting at the same table. She cuts out the shapes. She makes the copies at the library because the church printer is out again. She remembers that Marcus has not been in three weeks and quietly asks his grandmother if everything is alright.

She has been doing this for years. She has never once had a curriculum team.

Here is what the school across the street has that she does not.

It has a whole department for this. Curriculum specialists. Materials that come ready to teach, at three reading levels, in the box. Software that tracks who is falling behind and flags it. A team whose entire job is to make one teacher's Sunday easier. That work is not more faithful than hers. It is just resourced.

She can have that help too. For free. On the phone in her apron pocket while the copies run.

She can take this week's lesson and get it written two ways, one for the little ones and one for the older kids, so nobody is lost and nobody is bored. She can make a coloring page and a simple activity for the story she is teaching. She can draft the note to the parents about next month's program. She can turn her attendance notebook into a simple sheet that shows her who she has not seen in a while, so no child slips away unnoticed.

Not to replace what she does. The teaching is hers. The love for those kids is hers. It gives her back her Saturday night and makes sure every child in that room is reached.

That is the gap. It was never that the church taught its children less carefully than the school. It is that nobody who sits in her pew, in a ministry she has carried alone, ever told her the tool was hers to use.

The Plug AI brings real AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Harvey. Matteson. Dolton. Not a webinar. A room. Real problems. Her actual lesson book open on the table.

The people raising the next generation on a volunteer's Saturday night are not behind. They were just never handed the tools the funded classrooms take for granted.

We are here to hand them over.

The plug is in.
