# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-08-06
# Angle: The transportation ministry / church van driver. The volunteer who gets the elderly and the carless to church every Sunday, running the whole pickup route from memory with no dispatch, no logistics software, no team. AI as the fleet-and-logistics help a big-budget church pays a whole department for.

---

Sunday morning starts at six for him, in a fifteen-passenger van, on the far side of Harvey, at the curb of a woman who has not driven in eleven years.

He is the transportation ministry. All of it. There is no department. There is him, one van, and a route he has never once written down.

So every week he carries the whole thing in his head. He knows Sister Ruthie needs the front seat because of her hip. He knows the two boys on Wood Street will not be ready and he builds the extra five minutes in without being asked. He knows which porch has no rail and which door to knock on twice. He works out the order so nobody with a walker is the last one riding around for forty minutes. He gets fourteen people who have no other way to church into a pew before the first song, and he is back out for the second load before anyone claps.

He has been the reason those members still come for years. He has never once had a dispatcher.

Here is what the church with the budget has that he does not.

It has a whole logistics operation. Routing software. A fleet coordinator. A sign-up system where riders confirm the night before. Somebody whose only job is to make sure the van is full, on time, and nobody gets left on a porch. That work is not more faithful than his. It is just resourced.

He can have that help too. For free. On the same phone already full of everybody's number.

He can hand it the list of names and addresses and get back a pickup order that does not send him crossing the same street three times. He can get one text that goes to every rider the night before instead of calling fourteen people one at a time. He can log who needs the front seat, who uses a walker, whose porch has no rail, so it is written down somewhere other than his own memory. He can plan the second run before he finishes the first. He can leave a route somebody else could actually drive the Sunday he is sick.

Not to replace what he does. The knowing is his. He is the one who learned that porch, that hip, that door you knock on twice. No machine rode that route for eleven years. It gives him back the early mornings and makes sure the day he cannot drive, Sister Ruthie still gets picked up.

That is the gap. It was never that his people needed church less than the ones who could drive themselves. It is that nobody who rode in that van, in a ministry he has carried alone, ever told him the tool was his to use.

The Plug AI brings real AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Harvey. Matteson. Dolton. Not a webinar. A room. Real problems. His actual route open on the table.

The people getting the forgotten to church on a volunteer's early Sunday are not behind. They were just never handed the tools the funded ministries take for granted.

We are here to hand them over.

The plug is in.
