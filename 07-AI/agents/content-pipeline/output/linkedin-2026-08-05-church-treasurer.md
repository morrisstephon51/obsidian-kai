# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-08-05
# Angle: The church treasurer. The volunteer who keeps the small church's books, counts the offering, and gets the giving statements out at year end. AI as the finance help the big church pays a firm for.

---

Somebody in your church knows exactly how much is in the account. To the dollar.

She is the treasurer. Nobody voted for the job in a real sense. She was good with numbers, so it became hers, and it has been hers for years.

She counts the offering on Sunday after everyone goes home. She keeps the books. She stands up at the annual meeting and explains where the money went, and she braces for the one person who always has a question. In January she types the giving statements so every family can file their taxes, and she does it by hand, one at a time, because that is how it has always been done.

She is careful. She is honest to a fault. And she carries a quiet fear that one day she will make a mistake in front of the whole congregation.

Here is what the big church across town has that she does not.

It has a bookkeeper. Maybe a firm. Someone paid to make the numbers clean and the report simple. That help is not more faithful. It is just resourced.

She can have that help too. For free. On the phone already in her hand.

She can drop in a messy list of numbers and get back a summary she can read out loud. She can ask it to turn the budget into plain language a fifteen year old could follow. She can have it draft the year end giving letters once and send the whole batch, instead of typing each one at midnight.

Not to replace her judgment. She still signs off. It gives her back the nights.

That is the gap. It was never that the small church handled money worse. It is that nobody who sits in her pew, in a room she already trusts, ever showed her the tool was hers to use.

The Plug AI brings real AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Harvey. Matteson. Dolton. Not a webinar. A room. Real problems. The actual spreadsheet open on the laptop.

The people who keep the church running are not behind. They were just never handed the tools the big rooms take for granted.

We are here to hand them over.

The plug is in.
