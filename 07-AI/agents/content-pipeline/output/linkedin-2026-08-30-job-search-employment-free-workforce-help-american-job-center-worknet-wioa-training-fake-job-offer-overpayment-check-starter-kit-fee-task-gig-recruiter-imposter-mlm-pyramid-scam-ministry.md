There is a young mother in that congregation who was up past midnight last night on her phone, applying to everything. She is thirty-four. The fast ones. "Congratulations, you're hired!" — no interview, just a text. "We'll send you a check to buy your home-office equipment, just deposit it and send back the difference." "Pay a small onboarding and training fee to secure your position." "Earn $300 a day from your phone doing simple tasks — just make a small deposit to unlock the higher-paying ones." To a woman who needs work tonight, not next month, those messages do not read like traps. They read like the yes she has been begging the universe for. They read like relief.

You know her.
