**Scam spine:** Voucher applicants and holders are hunted with a special cruelty, because two facts about them are visible from a mile off: they are **desperate for a door** after years on a list, and they believe — because no one ever explained otherwise — that the system is a black box where **money or connections make things happen faster.** Every scam here weaponizes that belief. **(1) The "pay a fee to RELEASE / ACTIVATE your approved voucher" scam:** a text, call, or letter says you've been **selected** and must pay a **"processing," "activation," "release," or "hold-your-spot" fee** — fast, by gift card or app — or lose it. **The housing authority never charges you a fee to issue, release, or activate a voucher. There is no such thing as a release fee.** The message that demands one is the scam, no matter how much it "knows" about you (your name, your kids, your application year are cheaply bought or scraped). **(2) The "jump the Section 8 waitlist for $X" mill:** an outfit — a **"Priority Placement Housing," a "housing placement service," a man at a folding table** — promises to **register you, move you up, or get you approved** for a fee. **Waitlists are free, and your place is set by lottery or by date-and-time — nobody can sell you a spot or move you up.** Anyone charging to "get you on" or "move you up" is selling air. **(3) The phantom "voucher-welcome" rental listing:** a too-good unit — three bedrooms, nice suburb, cheap, **"Section 8 OK"** — with a landlord who's **"out of town" or "relocated"** and wants **first month, deposit, or an "application fee" wired or by Zelle/Cash App to "hold it"** before you can see it or sign anything. **Real landlords and the voucher program do not take your money before you tour the unit, before an inspection, and before a lease.** The unit doesn't exist, or isn't theirs. **(4) The housing-authority IMPOSTER "pay to keep your voucher" call:** a caller "from the housing authority" says your voucher is **being terminated,** or that you must **"reverify" by giving your Social Security number and bank details,** or **pay a fine to reinstate** — right now. **The real PHA communicates important actions in writing, holds hearings, and never demands gift cards, wires, or immediate payment to keep your voucher.** That call is theft wearing the authority's name. **(5) The illegal landlord SIDE PAYMENT:** after you're approved, a landlord asks you to pay **cash "under the table" above the approved rent,** or a **"key fee," "finder's fee," or extra deposit** off the books. **Side payments are a program violation — they can cost you your voucher and leave you with no legal protection for a dime of it.** A landlord who demands one is putting *your* subsidy at risk to line his pocket. **(6) The gift-card / money-app / wire cash-out:** the finish line of all of the above — because those payments are **instant and irreversible,** which is the whole reason a thief wants them for something a government program provides for free. Every one of these turns the honest hope of *finally getting my kids a door that locks* into the moment a working family is robbed of its cushion, its place in line, or the voucher itself. **The housing authority never charges to give you or keep a voucher; nobody can sell you a spot on the list; no real landlord takes your money before a tour, an inspection, and a lease; and no side payment is ever safe. The fee, the "release," the wire before the walkthrough, and the cash "on top" are the tells.**

**Key facts:**
- **You apply to a housing authority's waitlist for FREE — and your place is by lottery or date-and-time, not by payment.** Lists **open and close;** check the **official PHA site** (in suburban Cook County, **thehacc.org;** in Chicago, **thecha.org**) for when a list is open and how to apply. You can be on **more than one** PHA's list. **Nobody can move you up, and no one legitimate charges to "register" you.**
- **There is NO fee to be selected, "release," or "activate" a voucher.** When your name comes up, the PHA **contacts you (in writing), holds a briefing,** and **issues the voucher** — free. Any text or call demanding a **"processing/activation/release fee"** is a scam, even if it knows your name and your kids' names.
- **After you get the voucher, you have a search period — and the unit gets inspected BEFORE you move in and pay.** You find a place, you and the landlord submit a **Request for Tenancy Approval,** and the PHA does a **Housing Quality Standards (HQS) inspection** and a **rent-reasonableness check** *before* the lease and the assistance start. **Your only money is your share of the rent plus a normal security deposit paid to the landlord after the lease — never a wire to "hold" a place you haven't seen.**
- **You can move your voucher — "portability."** A Housing Choice Voucher can generally be **taken to almost any jurisdiction in the country.** There can be rules and a short waiting period at first, but **you request it in writing from your housing authority — you never buy portability from a stranger.**
- **You keep the voucher by RECERTIFYING every year and reporting changes on time — and honesty protects you.** Once a year the PHA **reexamines your income and household;** you also report changes in between. **If your income drops, reporting it can LOWER your rent share — reporting the truth on time protects you; silence is what puts a voucher at risk.**
- **You have the right to written notice and a hearing before a voucher is terminated.** The PHA cannot end your voucher with a **phone call demanding payment.** If you get a real termination notice, call **free legal aid (CARPLS 312-738-9200 / Legal Aid Chicago)** and ask for an **informal hearing.**
- **A landlord generally cannot refuse you just for having a voucher, and cannot make you pay "on the side."** In **Illinois and Cook County, source of income is protected** — report refusals to **IDHR / HUD FHEO 1-800-669-9777 / South Suburban Housing Center.** And **never pay cash above the approved rent** — a side payment is illegal and can cost you the voucher.
- **How to start (free):** confirm your status **only through the official PHA site/number** (**thehacc.org**), never through a texted link; if a list is open, **apply free;** get a **free HUD-approved housing counselor (1-800-569-4287)** to walk you through the search, inspection, portability, and recertification; and report any **fee demand or phantom listing** to **reportfraud.ftc.gov** and **IL AG 1-800-386-5438.**

"This is the part that protects you from the *other* scam," Ola Mae said, "the pretty apartment online. "So if you ever see a listing — three bedrooms, nice block, cheap, 'Section 8 welcome' — and the landlord says he's *out of town,* can't show it, but if you **wire the deposit** or send first month on the cash app he'll **mail you the keys** — Terrance, that is a ghost. "First: one day somebody may call, sounding *very* official, and say **'this is the housing authority, your voucher is being terminated, pay a fine to reinstate,'** or **'give me your Social and your bank number to reverify or you lose it.'** Baby, no. **A real housing authority cannot take your voucher with a phone call.** They act **in writing,** and if they ever move to end a voucher, **you have the right to a notice and a hearing** — and you call free legal aid, CARPLS, Legal Aid Chicago, and you *fight* it with somebody in your corner.
