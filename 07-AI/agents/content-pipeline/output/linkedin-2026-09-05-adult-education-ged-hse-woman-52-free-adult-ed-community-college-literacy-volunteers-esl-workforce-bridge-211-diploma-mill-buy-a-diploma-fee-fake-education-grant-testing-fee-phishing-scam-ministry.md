She's fifty-two. She left school somewhere in her teens — a baby, a sick parent, a paycheck the family needed that year — and she has worked and raised people ever since, and she never once got to walk back and finish. At work the promotion she has earned in every way but one says "high school diploma or equivalent required," and it goes to somebody younger who has the paper she doesn't.
