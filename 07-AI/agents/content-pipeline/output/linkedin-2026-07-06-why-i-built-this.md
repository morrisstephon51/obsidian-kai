# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI

I grew up in church.

I know what it looks like when the pastor has to be everything. Counselor. Advocate. Community navigator. The person you call when the system fails you.

I also know what it looks like when the community falls behind on something important and nobody in the building knows how to close the gap.

AI is moving fast.

The neighborhoods I grew up in are not moving with it. Not because the people are not capable. Because no one is bringing the right information into the right spaces.

That is the whole reason I built The Plug AI.

AI literacy workshops. In faith communities. Starting in south suburban Cook County, Chicago.

Not because churches are the only answer. Because churches are where the trust already exists. Where people show up. Where someone has already earned the right to say "let me show you something."

I am looking for three churches in the south suburbs who want to be the first to host a free AI literacy workshop this fall.

No tech background required. We bring the materials. We lead the session. Your congregation leaves knowing more than they did when they walked in.

If that sounds like your church, I want to hear from you.

The plug is in.
