# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-08-04
# Angle: The church administrator. The one volunteer who runs the whole small-church office alone after her own workday. AI as the assistant the big church down the street already has on staff.

---

Every small church has one person who holds the whole thing together.

She is not on the payroll. She has a job of her own that ends at five, and then the church work begins.

The bulletin. The newsletter nobody else remembers to write. The list of who is sick, who lost someone, who needs a meal this week. The funeral program typed at two in the morning because the service is tomorrow and the family is grieving and someone has to do it.

She does it because she loves the church. She has done it for years. And she is tired in a way nobody sees.

Here is what the big church across town has that she does not.

It has a staff. A communications person. Someone whose whole job is the newsletter and the graphics and the schedule. That help is not holier. It is just resourced.

She can have that help too. For free. On the phone already in her hand.

She can hand it the pastor's rough notes and get a clean bulletin back. She can ask it to write the newsletter in her own voice and then fix the parts that are not quite right. She can turn a long announcement into something short enough that people actually read it.

Not to replace her. To give her back the hours she keeps giving away.

That is the gap. It was never that the small church mattered less. It is that nobody who sits in her pew, in a room she already trusts, ever showed her the tool was hers to use.

The Plug AI brings real AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Harvey. Matteson. Dolton. Not a webinar. A room. Real problems. The actual bulletin open on the laptop.

The people who keep the church running are not behind. They were just never handed the tools the big rooms take for granted.

We are here to hand them over.

The plug is in.
