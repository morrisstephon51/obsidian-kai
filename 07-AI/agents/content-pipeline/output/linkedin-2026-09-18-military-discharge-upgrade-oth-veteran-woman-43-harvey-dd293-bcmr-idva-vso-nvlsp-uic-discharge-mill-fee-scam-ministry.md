**Here is what Tamika didn't know — and what your veterans need to know:**

**1. Discharge upgrades are applied for using free government forms.**
The DD-293 goes to the Discharge Review Board (DRB), which can review discharges within 15 years of the discharge date. The DD-149 goes to the Board for Correction of Military Records (BCMR) and has no time limit. Both forms are free. Both processes are free. Both are submitted directly to the Department of Defense. You do not need to pay anyone to file them.

**2. The 2017 DoD guidance changed the calculus for PTSD, MST, and TBI cases.**
In 2016 and 2017, the Department of Defense issued guidance directing all service Discharge Review Boards and Boards for Correction of Military Records to give "liberal consideration" to upgrade applications where PTSD, military sexual trauma, or traumatic brain injury may have been a contributing factor to the misconduct that led to discharge. This doesn't mean automatic approval. It means the board must genuinely consider the connection. For veterans like Tamika — whose behavior after deployment clearly tracked a PTSD pattern — this guidance opened a door that was previously closed. If you were discharged before 2017 and never knew this guidance existed, you may have a viable case that was never evaluated fairly.

**3. What an upgrade can unlock:**
An upgrade from OTH to General (Under Honorable Conditions) or Honorable can restore access to:
- VA healthcare enrollment
- VA disability compensation claims (retroactive to the upgrade date)
- GI Bill education benefits (Post-9/11 or Montgomery)
- VA home loan guaranty
- Veterans pension eligibility

This is not small. For a veteran who served in a combat zone and has untreated service-connected conditions, this can mean healthcare access, monthly income, and housing support that changes a life.

**4. Free help is real and available in Cook County and the South Suburbs.**
You do not need a paid consultant. You need an accredited representative:

- **Cook County Veterans Assistance Commission** — free VSO services; offices across the county; find your township's representative at cookcountyil.gov/agency/veterans-assistance-commission
- **Illinois Department of Veterans' Affairs (IDVA)** — (800) 437-9824 / veterans.illinois.gov — free Veterans Service Representatives statewide; they know the discharge upgrade process
- **National Veterans Legal Services Program** — nvlsp.org — free legal representation for discharge upgrades, especially PTSD/MST/TBI cases
- **Veterans' Legal Support Center & Clinic at UIC John Marshall Law School** — (312) 563-8032 — free legal services for Illinois veterans, including records correction and discharge upgrades
- **American Legion / VFW / DAV** — all have accredited service officers who file DD-293 and DD-149 packages at no charge; find your local post
- **Swords to Plowshares** — national veterans legal organization, strong track record with PTSD-linked discharges

**5. No private company can guarantee an upgrade. Anyone who does is lying.**
The DoD board makes an independent decision based on your record, supporting documentation, and current guidance. A legitimate representative helps you build the strongest possible case. A scam consultant charges you thousands for the same free forms, adds inflated promises, and disappears regardless of outcome.

**6. Veterans Crisis Line: 988, then press 1.**
For any veteran navigating a discharge upgrade process who is also managing mental health — this number is always free, always confidential, always available.
