# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-08-06
# Angle: The minister of music / choir director. The volunteer who builds the whole worship service by ear every week, runs rehearsal, keeps the choir together, and never had a music department. AI as the worship-production help a big-budget church pays a whole team for.

---

On Thursday night, there is a man at an old upright piano teaching four alto parts by ear, one phrase at a time, because there is no sheet music and there never was.

He is the minister of music. He plays by ear. He hears the whole arrangement in his head and pulls it out of the choir one voice at a time.

So every week he does the whole job. He picks the songs that fit the sermon he has not been told about yet. He figures out what key the lead singer can actually reach. He writes the words out by hand so the older members can read them. He texts fifteen people about rehearsal. He learns the new song off his phone in the car. He shows up Sunday and holds it all together while it looks effortless.

He has been carrying the music of that church for years. He has never once had a team.

Here is what the church downtown has that he does not.

It has a whole production department. A worship pastor. Musicians who get paid. Software that prints the chord charts, transposes the key with one tap, and lays out the whole run of service. People whose only job is to make Sunday morning easy for the person up front. That work is not more anointed than his. It is just resourced.

He can have that help too. For free. On the same phone he already learns songs on.

He can hand it the song and get a clean lyric and chord sheet the choir can actually read. He can move a song into the singer's key without sitting at the piano working it out by hand. He can lay out the order of service and get it to the sound booth before Sunday. He can draft the one text that goes to every musician instead of fifteen separate ones. He can take a hymn nobody knows and get the parts broken down simply enough to teach on Thursday.

Not to replace what he does. The ear is his. The feel is his. Nothing he pulls out of that choir comes from a machine. It gives him back the hours and lets the music breathe.

That is the gap. It was never that the church worshiped with less skill than the one with the budget. It is that nobody who sits under his music, in a ministry he has carried alone, ever told him the tool was his to use.

The Plug AI brings real AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Harvey. Matteson. Dolton. Not a webinar. A room. Real problems. His actual song list open on the piano.

The people carrying worship on a volunteer's Thursday night are not behind. They were just never handed the tools the funded sanctuaries take for granted.

We are here to hand them over.

The plug is in.
