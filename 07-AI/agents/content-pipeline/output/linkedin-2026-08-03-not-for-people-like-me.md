# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-08-03
# Angle: The elder who says "AI is not for people like me." She has already been using it. She just was never told.

---

"That is not for people like me."

An older woman said that to me after service. She meant AI. She was sure it belonged to someone else. Younger. Richer. Somewhere far from her block.

Here is what I told her.

You already use it.

When your phone finishes your sentence, that is AI. When the bank texts you about a charge you did not make, that is AI. When your grandson's music app knows the next song, that is AI.

She has been using it for years. Nobody ever said the word out loud to her.

That is the gap. It was never about ability. It was about who gets shown and who gets left out.

So we showed her one thing. How to ask a question in plain words and get a plain answer back. A letter she could not read. A bill she did not understand. A form nobody explained.

She did it herself. In the room. First try.

The look on her face was not amazement at the machine.

It was the look of someone realizing a door she thought was locked was open the whole time.

The Plug AI brings real AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Harvey. Matteson. Dolton. Not a webinar. A room. Real problems. People who look like the people we serve.

AI was never only for people far from your block.

The plug is in.
