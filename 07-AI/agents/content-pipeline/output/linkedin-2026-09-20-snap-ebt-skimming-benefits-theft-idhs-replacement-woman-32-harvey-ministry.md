**Scam spine:** EBT card skimming is a documented and growing form of benefits theft in Illinois. Thieves attach electronic skimming devices to card readers at gas stations, grocery store self-checkouts, and convenience stores — capturing the card's magnetic stripe data and PIN. They clone the card and drain the balance, often overnight, often in a different state. Victims discover a zero balance and no food for the week. The legitimate path to recovery is a free SNAP benefits replacement claim through IDHS, mandated under federal law since 2023. What has grown alongside that free pathway is a shadow industry of fake "recovery services" — Facebook groups, Instagram pages, and WhatsApp contacts offering to "expedite" the IDHS process for a fee of $60 to $150. These services do nothing except collect money. IDHS processes replacement claims based on its own investigation — no private party accelerates that process. A family that pays $60 to a "Benefits Coordinator IL" on Facebook receives nothing it would not have received by calling 1-800-843-6154 directly, on roughly the same timeline. They are out the sixty dollars on top of the stolen $387.

**Key legal facts:**
- **Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2023 (P.L. 117-328), Section 4005:** required states to replace SNAP benefits stolen through card skimming, cloning, and phishing; Illinois participates in the replacement program; states were required to implement replacement processes and Illinois IDHS has maintained and continued replacement processing following the initial federal mandate period
- **What Illinois replaces:** IDHS replaces SNAP benefits stolen through unauthorized electronic transactions that the household did not authorize; the replacement covers the stolen amount up to a maximum of two months of the household's monthly allotment within a 12-month period; larger-scale ongoing theft may require escalation
- **How to file:** contact your local FCRC in person or call 1-800-843-6154; report the unauthorized transactions (dates, amounts, locations if visible in your history), confirm that you did not share your card or PIN, and provide your name, case number, and contact information; IDHS opens an investigation and, if the claim is validated, issues a replacement credit to the EBT account; no fee at any stage
- **How skimming works:** thieves install a physical skimmer — a thin reader that captures magnetic stripe data — inside a card reader slot or over a keypad; the skimmer records the card number and PIN as the victim uses the terminal; a second device transmits or stores the data; the thief encodes a cloned card with the stolen credentials and drains the balance, frequently from a remote location within hours; victims have no way to know this has occurred until they check their balance
- **Magnetic stripe vulnerability:** EBT cards issued by most states, including Illinois, use magnetic stripe technology rather than chip-and-PIN; magnetic stripe data is trivially copyable by a skimmer in a way that chip data is not; federal discussion of transitioning EBT to chip technology has continued, but as of 2026 most Illinois EBT cards remain magnetic-stripe-based
- **No private party accelerates IDHS investigation:** IDHS replacement investigations are managed by agency staff; the processing timeline depends on caseload, investigator capacity, and the clarity of the transaction anomaly in the record; no outside person has authority to prioritize individual claims; anyone claiming a "contact at IDHS" who can expedite a claim is either deceiving the claimant or misrepresenting how the agency works; payment does not change the investigation timeline
- **Organized skimming as federal crime:** large-scale EBT skimming operations are prosecuted federally as wire fraud, benefits fraud, and conspiracy; the FBI and USDA Office of Inspector General (OIG) investigate and bring charges against organized rings; individual victims are not parties to those prosecutions but can report their compromised transactions to USDA OIG at 1-800-424-9121
- **Illinois AG consumer fraud authority:** charging fees while misrepresenting the ability to provide services already available for free through a government agency may constitute consumer fraud under the Illinois Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act (815 ILCS 505); fake SNAP "recovery" services are subject to AG enforcement; report at illinoisattorneygeneral.gov or 1-800-243-0618

**Here is what happened to your EBT card — and what Illinois will actually do about it:**

**1. Your EBT was cloned. This is a specific crime with a specific remedy.**

EBT cards in Illinois use magnetic stripes, not chips. Magnetic stripe data can be captured by a skimmer — a thin device installed inside the card reader slot at a gas pump, a grocery store self-checkout, or a convenience store terminal. The skimmer reads the card number. A compromised keypad overlay captures the PIN. The thief encodes that data onto a blank card and spends your balance in a different city, often before dawn.

You don't know it happened until you check your account.

This is organized theft. It runs at high frequency along specific corridors in the south suburbs. The same gas station skimmer that hit Tamara's card hit three other families from the same congregation within the same two-week window. When IDHS investigated, the pattern was visible in the data within hours of the inquiry being opened.

**2. Illinois will replace your stolen benefits. It is the law. It is free.**

Under the Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2023, Illinois is required to replace SNAP benefits stolen through card skimming and cloning. IDHS manages the replacement claim process.

Here is what you do:

Call your local IDHS Family Community Resource Center or 1-800-843-6154.

Say: *"My EBT card was compromised. I have unauthorized transactions I did not make. I want to file a replacement claim."*

Provide: the dates and amounts of the unauthorized transactions, the locations where they occurred if visible in your history, your name, and your IDHS case number. Your FCRC will open the claim. IDHS investigates. If the claim is validated — and geographically anomalous transactions in another state are strong evidence — IDHS issues a replacement credit to your account.

This costs nothing. No form-filing fee. No expediting fee. No attorney. No intermediary.

**3. The Facebook "coordinator" does nothing that costs sixty dollars.**

There is no private pathway into IDHS claim processing. No outside person has standing to prioritize your investigation. The timeline is what the timeline is — and it is the same whether you call IDHS yourself or whether you pay someone on Facebook who then calls IDHS on your behalf.

What the "Benefits Coordinator" does in practice:
- Collects $60 to $100 via Zelle or Cash App
- Possibly calls IDHS and reports the same information you could have reported yourself, for free
- In many cases, stops responding within a day or two
- Changes nothing about the investigation timeline

The five families from Greater Mount Calvary who paid received the same outcome — replacement credit in roughly two to three weeks — as the families who called IDHS directly. They were out the fee on top of the theft.

**4. While you wait: the pantry is the bridge.**

Replacement claims take time. Families need food now.

Church food pantries exist for exactly this gap. A family does not stop needing groceries because an IDHS investigation takes two to three weeks.

If you are a south suburban family whose EBT benefits were stolen and a replacement claim is pending, call your church food pantry coordinator before you decide you have no options. That is what the ministry is there for.

**5. Protect yourself going forward.**

- **Check your balance often.** The faster you catch unauthorized transactions, the cleaner the replacement claim. Check at minimum every few days — not just at the beginning of the month.
- **Cover the keypad when you enter your PIN.** Keypad overlays are part of skimming infrastructure. A hand over the pad when you type defeats the PIN capture.
- **Pull the card reader panel at gas pumps.** A skimmer installed at a pump is often visible as a slight looseness or misalignment in the reader panel. If it moves when you tug it, don't swipe there.
- **Report immediately.** A zero balance is not a bank error that corrects itself. Call IDHS the same day you see the unauthorized transactions.
