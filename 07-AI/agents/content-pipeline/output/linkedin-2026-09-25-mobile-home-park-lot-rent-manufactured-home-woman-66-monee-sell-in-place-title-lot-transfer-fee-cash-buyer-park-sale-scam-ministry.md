**Scam spine:** Manufactured-home owners in parks are hunted with a special, cold precision, because two facts about them are visible from the highway: they own a **home they cannot easily move** (relocating a manufactured home can cost thousands and many homes can't survive the move at all), and they have usually **never been told they have any rights** to the ground under it. That combination — a trapped, valuable asset and an owner who thinks she's powerless — is a predator's dream, and every scam here works it. **(1) The "pay a fee to KEEP your home on the lot" scam (lot-transfer / title-registration / re-registration fee):** after a park sells, residents get a **letter, text, or call** demanding a **"one-time fee"** to "transfer the lot agreement," "register your home with new ownership," or "keep your title current" — pay fast, by gift card or app, **"or your home is abandoned and will be removed."** There is **no such fee.** A new park owner steps into the **same lease and the same law;** they cannot charge you to keep a home **you already own** on a lot you already rent, and they cannot make your home "abandoned" with a text and a countdown. **(2) The predatory "we're the only buyer the park will approve — sell cheap TODAY" cash-buyer:** a flyer or knock — **"WE BUY MOBILE HOMES CASH"** — from someone who says the new owner **won't approve any other buyer,** that a big fee or removal is coming, and that you should **sign the title over now** for a lowball price (a fraction of the home's worth), often with a **"title-clearing" or "processing" fee** you pay *them.* This is how paid-off homes get stolen for pennies — you have the **right to sell your home in place** to any qualified buyer, and fear is the only reason to sell it cheap to the first vulture at the door. **(3) The illegal "exit / transfer fee" or forced commission:** a shady park tells you that to sell your home in place you must pay the park an **"exit fee," "transfer fee,"** or a **commission on the sale** — or remove the home entirely. **Illinois law protects your right to sell in place;** many such fees and demands are **not lawful,** and legal aid can tell you which. **(4) The new-owner / park-manager IMPOSTER "or we haul it tomorrow" threat:** a caller "from the new management" demands **back lot rent, a fee, or your Social Security and bank number** by **gift card, Zelle, or wire, right now,** or the home gets removed and the sheriff called. **A real park cannot end your tenancy or remove your home with a phone call** — that takes proper written notice and, if it comes to it, a court process with your right to be heard. **(5) The fake "mobile-home repair grant" or "lot-rent assistance grant" fee:** a promise of grant money for repairs or to cover a rent jump — if you first pay a **"processing" or "application" fee.** Real assistance never charges you a fee to apply. **(6) The gift-card / money-app / wire / title-signature cash-out:** the finish line of all of the above — because a **gift card, a Zelle, a wire, and a signed-over title** are **instant and irreversible,** which is the whole reason a thief wants them for a "fee" a legitimate landlord would never charge and a home a legitimate buyer would never lowball. Every one of these turns the honest fear of *losing the home I already paid for* into the moment a fixed-income owner is robbed of a furnace fund, a home's worth of equity, or the home itself. **A new owner cannot charge you to keep what you own; you have the right to sell your home in place; no fee "registers" or "transfers" a title you already hold; and no phone call hauls off your house. The fee, the "abandonment" countdown, the wire, and the "sign here today" are the tells.**

**Key facts:**
- **You own the HOME; you rent the LOT — and that gives you rights under a specific Illinois law.** The **Mobile Home Landlord and Tenant Rights Act (765 ILCS 745)** protects people who own their manufactured/mobile home and rent the lot. A new owner who buys the park **steps into the same lease and the same law** — buying the ground does **not** let them charge you to keep a home you already own.
- **There is NO "lot-transfer" or "title-registration" fee you pay to a park owner to keep your home.** A text or letter demanding a fee — "or your home is abandoned and will be removed" — is a **scam,** even if it knows your lot number, your home's year, or your late spouse's name (that information is cheap to find). No legitimate owner registers or activates your ownership for a fee.
- **The park must generally offer you a WRITTEN LEASE and give ADVANCE WRITTEN NOTICE before raising rent or changing the rules.** A rent jump with almost no notice, or a "pay now or lose the home" ultimatum, is **not** how the law works. Get every notice **in writing** and take it to **free legal aid (Prairie State Legal Services in Will/Kankakee County)** before you pay or agree to anything.
- **You have the RIGHT TO SELL YOUR HOME IN PLACE.** You can sell your manufactured home and leave it on the lot to a buyer who qualifies as a new tenant; the park generally **cannot force you to remove it just to sell,** and illegal **"exit/transfer fees" or forced commissions** may not be lawful. So **"we're the only buyer the park will approve, sell cheap today"** is a lie built to steal your equity out of fear.
- **Your home has a TITLE, through the Illinois Secretary of State — protect it.** **Never sign your title over** to a cash buyer on a threat, and **never pay a "title-clearing / registration" fee** to keep a title you already hold. Real title transfer happens through the **SOS,** on your terms, after legal aid has looked at the deal.
- **A park CANNOT make your home "abandoned" and haul it off with a text and a countdown.** Ending a mobile-home tenancy or removing a home requires **proper written notice** and, if contested, a **court process** where you have the right to be heard. Free legal aid can fight a removal or eviction — call the day a notice arrives.
- **If the park ever really CLOSES or changes use, Illinois law requires substantial advance written notice** — and there may be help — but **you never pay a stranger a fee to "get relocation assistance."** Take any closure notice to **legal aid and the Attorney General 1-800-386-5438** immediately.
- **How to protect yourself (free):** verify anything a "new owner" claims **in person at the real park office and in writing;** confirm the park is **licensed with IDPH;** call **Prairie State Legal Services** about any notice, fee, sale, or removal threat; use the **SOS** for title questions; and report fee demands and predatory buyers to **the IL AG (1-800-386-5438)** and **FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov).** Never send a **gift card, Zelle, or wire,** and never **sign a title,** on a deadline.
