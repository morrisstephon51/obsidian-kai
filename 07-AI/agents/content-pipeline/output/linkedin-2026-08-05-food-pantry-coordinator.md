# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-08-05
# Angle: The food pantry / benevolence coordinator. The volunteer who runs the church pantry, meets people on their worst week, and holds the list of where to send them. AI as the case-management help a funded agency pays staff for.

---

Every church has one closet that says more about it than the sanctuary does.

It is the food pantry. And somebody runs it.

She is the one who unlocks the door on the day the line forms. She knows which shelf is low. She knows the family that comes in dressed like nothing is wrong, and she knows better. She meets people on the worst week of their year and she never lets them feel it.

She also keeps a list in her head. The clinic that will see you without insurance. The number for the light bill help before it gets shut off. Which township office to call, and which one will just leave you on hold.

She is not paid for any of this. She does it after her real job.

Here is what the funded agency across town has that she does not.

It has case managers. Intake software. A staff person whose whole job is to hand you a clean sheet of where to go next. That help is not more compassionate than hers. It is just resourced.

She can have that help too. For free. On the phone already in her hand.

She can ask it to turn the flyer for Saturday's distribution into something people will actually read. She can build a simple intake sheet in five minutes instead of borrowing one that never fit. She can take everything she keeps in her head, the clinics and the utility help and the numbers, and get back a clean one-page resource list she can print and hand across the table. She can even draft the paragraph for the small grant that keeps the shelves full.

Not to replace what she does. The trust is hers. It gives her back the hours.

That is the gap. It was never that the church pantry cared less. It is that nobody who stands in that line, in a room she already runs, ever showed her the tool was hers to use.

The Plug AI brings real AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Harvey. Matteson. Dolton. Not a webinar. A room. Real problems. The actual flyer open on the laptop.

The people holding a community together are not behind. They were just never handed the tools the funded rooms take for granted.

We are here to hand them over.

The plug is in.
