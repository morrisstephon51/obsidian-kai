**Here is what Jerome didn't know — and what you need to know:**

**1. Filing for SSDI is free. It always has been.**
You can apply at ssa.gov/disability, call (800) 772-1213, or walk into any Social Security field office. There is no filing fee. There is no processing fee. If anyone charges you money to apply, that is your first warning.

**2. Upfront fees from SSDI "advocates" are illegal under federal law.**
Approved Social Security representatives — attorneys or non-attorneys — may only collect payment if your case is approved, and only from past-due benefits. The fee is capped at 25% of back pay or $7,200, whichever is less. Nothing upfront. No exceptions. An "application preparation fee" is not legal in this context. If you paid one, report it.

**3. The initial denial rate for SSDI is roughly 65%. That doesn't mean your case is over.**
Most people who are ultimately approved are denied once or twice first. The appeals process — reconsideration, then a hearing before an Administrative Law Judge — is where most cases are won. A legitimate representative works the full appeals process at no cost to you upfront. An application mill files once, takes your money, and disappears.

**4. You have real free options in the South Suburbs and Chicago region:**

- **Prairie State Legal Services** — (708) 342-0011 — free legal help for disability applications and appeals for income-qualifying residents; offices serving Harvey, Joliet, Waukegan, and beyond.
- **Equip for Equality** — (800) 537-2632 / equipforequality.org — Illinois's federally designated disability rights organization; helps with SSDI denials and disability discrimination.
- **Land of Lincoln Legal Aid** — (800) 252-8005 — covers much of downstate and suburban Illinois with free civil legal services.
- **Illinois Legal Aid Online** — illinoislegalaid.org — step-by-step guides on SSDI appeals and attorney referral.

**5. If someone "guarantees" your approval, they're lying.**
No one can guarantee Social Security approval. An ALJ decides every hearing independently. Any representative using that language to close a sale is using sales tactics, not legal expertise.

**6. Report disability application fraud:**
FTC: reportfraud.ftc.gov
Illinois Attorney General Consumer Protection Hotline: 1-800-243-0618
