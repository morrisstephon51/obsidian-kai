# LinkedIn Post — The Plug AI / Faith Community Audience
# Date: 2026-08-04
# Angle: The first-gen student. The kid filling out college applications alone at the kitchen table because the adults who love them never got to do this. AI as the private tutor that families with money already pay for.

---

There is a kid in your congregation filling out a college application alone.

Not because nobody loves her. Because the people who love her never got to do this.

Her parents work hard. They are proud. And when she asks what a personal statement is, they go quiet, because they do not know either.

She is Googling "how to write about yourself" at eleven at night. She is looking at FAFSA like it is written in another language. She is guessing at questions that decide the next four years of her life.

Here is what the kid across town has that she does not.

He has a counselor his family pays for. Someone who reads his essay, tells him what is working, asks him better questions until the real story comes out. That help is not magic. It is just access.

She can have that too. For free. On the phone already in her hand.

She can paste her rough essay and ask what is strong and what is unclear. She can ask what a form is really asking before she fills it in. She can practice the hard interview questions out loud in her own kitchen until they stop being scary.

Not to write it for her. To sit with her while she writes it herself.

That is the gap. It was never that she was not smart enough. It is that nobody who looks like her, in a room she already trusts, ever showed her the tool was hers to use.

The Plug AI brings real AI literacy to faith communities in south suburban Cook County. Harvey. Matteson. Dolton. Not a webinar. A room. Real problems. The application open on the kitchen table.

First-generation is not a disadvantage. It is a first.

We are here to make sure she is not the last.

The plug is in.
