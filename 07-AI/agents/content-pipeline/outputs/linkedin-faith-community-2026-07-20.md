Your deacon applied for a job last week.

A human never read the resume.

An algorithm screened it based on keywords it was trained to look for. If the format was wrong or the words did not match exactly it went to the no pile.

Nobody in the congregation knew to warn him.

That is the gap.

Faith communities are trusted in a way tech companies will never be. When a pastor says "learn this" people listen. When a community health worker walks someone through a new tool they actually use it.

That trust is the most valuable asset in AI literacy work.

The Plug AI is bringing AI skills to south suburban Cook County. Starting in faith communities. Because the people the tech world forgot deserve to understand the tools already making decisions about their lives.

The plug is in.
