## 🚀 How to Use This

### Daily Practice (5-10 minutes)

**Each morning or evening:**

1. **Duplicate** `Templates/Daily Note.md` 
   - Name it: `Daily Notes/2026-06-12.md`
   - Fill in the date

2. **Fill sections:**
   - **🎯 Focus:** What matters today?
   - **📚 Learning:** What did you learn?
   - **🔄 Decisions:** What did you decide?
   - **💭 Reflections:** What's on your mind?

3. **Link to context:**
   - Link to relevant Area
   - Link to [[me]] or specific goal
   - Create connection to larger strategy

4. **Review in morning:**
   - Review yesterday's note
   - See what you learned
   - Build on momentum

### Weekly Review (15-20 minutes)

**Every Sunday or Monday:**

1. **Review daily notes** from the week
   - What patterns emerged?
   - What wins happened?
   - What challenges remain?

2. **Update relevant goal notes**
   - [[Areas/Career/Career Goals]] — Progress? Adjust?
   - [[Areas/Finance/Financial Goals]] — On track?
   - [[Areas/Education/Education Goals]] — Milestones?

3. **Check Area hubs**
   - [[Areas/Personal Growth/index|Personal Growth]]
   - [[Areas/Education/index|Education]]
   - [[Areas/Career/index|Career]]
   - [[Areas/Finance/index|Finance]]
   - [[Areas/Development/index|Development]]

### Monthly Review (30-45 minutes)

**Last Sunday of month:**

1. **Reflect on me.md**
   - Still accurate?
   - What's changed?
   - Any updates needed?

2. **Review all goal notes**
   - Update progress
   - Adjust targets
   - Celebrate wins

3. **Audit vault structure**
   - Any orphaned notes?
   - Any connections missing?
   - Archive completed projects

4. **Plan next month**
   - Top 3 priorities?
   - Which goal to focus on?
   - What do you need to learn?
